## The editorial correction

A voice-recovery edit is **not safe merely because the plot still works within its own chapter**. An apparently incidental sentence may introduce an image, habitual word, object, social gesture, false inference, physical fact, or unspoken obligation that matures chapters later. Likewise, a deliberately unremarkable line may create the contrast that makes a return painful.

Recover the author's **narrative voice** while retaining the book's **memory**.

The [Promise Ledger](../../Promise-Ledger.md) remains the single owner for book-wide setups and payoffs; this companion documents only how the **new comparison drafts** treat its established connections. Do not copy these tables into a parallel canonical ledger. The [Before-You-Touch-A-Chapter](../../Before-You-Touch-A-Chapter.md) ruling/lock sheet and [Decisions](../../Decisions.md) win over any suggestion below.

### Different kinds of connections — do not flatten them together

1. **Causal setup → consequence:** the first blob is present **before** the cookbook working; the later attacks make sense of why. Removing its timing would break information, not just mood.
2. **Verbal or visual echo:** the bare opening line returns in Ch8; the short readiness exchange returns **with agency reversed** in Ch18. Its power partly depends on *not pointing it out*.
3. **Prop/custody chain:** a cookbook, diary, ticket, spoon, foam dagger or archival record must remain in the right person's hands at the right time, be spent/donated or destroyed when shown, and recur without magical or physical resurrection.
4. **Character pattern and reversal:** Pathwell giving unasked-for answers, calling her Lizzy, offering constrained "choice," and treating cost as his to pay lead into later restraint, full confession and an invitation *he doesn't control*.
5. **Accumulating human texture:** bad coffee, repaired clothing, a child's sock and an archive catalogue make lives visible **before** those ordinary details are put at risk. Their value is not solely that they identify a later event.
6. **Unresolved, author-approved openness:** the exact identity/circumstances of the person Pathwell tried to help, parts of pruning mechanics, Mama Baga's lightly shown maternal role and who at Camp ever learns the confession are **not holes to fill automatically**.

**A repeated detail is not necessarily foreshadowing.** Some patterns are thematic resonance, ordinary world texture or family resemblance; avoid manufacturing a formal plot promise from every colorful sentence. Ask what the reader would actually be led to expect.

## Required check before selecting any recovered sentence

For every proposed insertion, cut, relocation, or changed gesture, record:

1. **Local function:** What does this line achieve *now*: image, staging, pleasure, pacing, intent, feeling?
2. **Backward link:** Is it returning a named, physical, tonal or rhetorical detail from earlier? Does the reader need precisely this word, object, action, speaker or timing?
3. **Forward link:** Does it quietly lay groundwork for a payoff, or unintentionally introduce a new expectation? Has the later scene still got room to change its meaning?
4. **Ledger status:** Find the corresponding [Promise Ledger](../../Promise-Ledger.md) P/O/R/L entry, or record "**texture only**" if it isn't a promise. Update that owner **only if a selected canon change actually alters** the plant or payoff.
5. **Custody/time/agency:** Who has the object, who knows the information, whose hand acts, who chose, and what cannot be in two places? Verify against both adjacent chapters and the payoff.
6. **Reader recognition:** Does the echo land without the narrator explaining it? Is a quiet repetition more moving than a highlighted reference?
7. **Author authority:** An expressive metaphor, joke, emotional resonance or final rhythm remains a candidate until chosen. No formulaic clue insertion, mandatory callback frequency or invented hidden significance.

**Never mark an echo preserved merely because a keyword appears somewhere in the draft.** A surface anchor comparison can catch deletions, but cannot verify subtext, emotional timing or the reader's unprimed understanding.
