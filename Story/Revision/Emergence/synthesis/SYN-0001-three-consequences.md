## Pieces being combined

### Piece A — Choice and character
Shade wants Camp to witness Pathwell's recklessness and abandonment, but ends up recognizing his own unchosen chance to act differently. By Q53, Pathwell's acknowledgment no longer matters to him.

### Piece B — Existing accident and rescue
Pathwell forces rejoining over Shade's refusal; failed working causes a lantern to ignite the archive. Elizabeth's choice to rescue Milo already exists, but the creature currently arrives afterward.

### Piece C — Cleanup and access
Shade is unresolved pruning waste, the creature follows his ambiguous signal, and failed working produces fresh spillage. Camp moves; accessibility slows cleanup. The author proposes a resisted crossing intensified by the new beacon.

## New combination
- combo: **One imposed solution, three independent consequences**: physical archive fire; stronger cleanup attraction; Shade's recognition that his fixation resembled the behavior he opposed. Elizabeth responds to an immediate human need rather than trying to control the magical catastrophe.

## What this makes possible
- opens: A convergent climax where plot mechanics, character arcs, and rescue choices collide without an extra invented villain, archive-arson mastermind or voluntary martyrdom. A warning near the boundary can heighten tension.

## Why this was not obvious before
- why-hidden: Prior Rung questions treated Shade's accusation, his blob encounter, Elizabeth's earlier rescue, Camp access, and the origin of the fire as separate problems. Connecting them exposed opportunities *and* important contradictions (especially the timeline).

## Risks or limits
- risk: In the manuscript Pathwell objectively starts the fire. Shade must not absorb blame for what Pathwell forced.
- risk: Simultaneity is untested; the author has not decided Shade's final physical behavior or Pathwell's sight line.
- risk: Non-resistance is only meaningful agency if readers can tell he could behave otherwise.
- risk: A thematic unity cannot substitute for Elizabeth materially changing a central outcome.
