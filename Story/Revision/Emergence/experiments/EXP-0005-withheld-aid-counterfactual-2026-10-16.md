# EXP-0005 — What actionable help did Pathwell withhold?

**Date:** 2026-10-16  
**Status:** TABLETOP CAUSAL REVIEW COMPLETE; **READER TEST OPEN / NOT PROMOTED**  
**Source:** Current [Ch14](../../../Chapters/Chapter_14.txt), [Ch15](../../../Chapters/Chapter_15.txt) and [Ch18](../../../Chapters/Chapter_18.txt), not an old Shade-voluntary scenario.

## Hypothesis
The current confession can be morally convincing without establishing **but for Pathwell's call, Shade would survive**. But it must still correspond to an action available in the scene, not a vacuous regret about speaking louder.

## Known situation (source-grounded)
1. Pathwell completes the forced paid working, is flung to the ground; the recoil lights the archive (Ch14).
2. Mama Baga directs people to fire response, the archivist seeks Milo, and Stansbury is approaching the other side of the wagon (Ch14–15).
3. The cleanup creature squeezes through Camp's boundary, grips Shade at his boot. Shade yells “Help!” (Ch15).
4. **One Camp man already hears or sees enough to run, grabs the jacket and pulls twice**; Shade can still speak and struggle (Ch15).
5. Pathwell is conscious, on his side, with face turned toward Shade, “close enough to hear” (Ch15).
6. Elizabeth enters the archive, frees Milo using an injured arm, and emerges after the creature has taken Shade (Ch15). The creature subsequently collects loose possibility.
7. Pathwell confesses he saw Shade, thought the creature would settle the unresolved problem, and did not call somebody (Ch18).

## Controlled counterfactuals

| Case | One change; everything else held | On-page basis | What can actually be concluded | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

| **F: Pathwell stays silent intentionally** | Hold current physical outcome, vary only Pathwell's motive | Ch18 supplies motive | This remains independently blameworthy as abandonment during a call for help, even if physical result unchanged | **PASS ethical counterfactual** |

## Result
**Do not add a magical solution.** The current version has a real moral failure and plausible crowd context, but the extra-help option is only minimally articulated. **B** is the only ordinary, potentially useful action whose participants already exist; it needs a viewpoint-safe and space-safe proof if a truly uninformed reader stops here. **E** needs location/timing validation before any wording. A revised line must not assert that his call would have saved Shade.

## Read-test protocol (not executed)
Give cold readers Ch1–18 without this hypothesis. After Ch15 ask “Who might have helped Shade, and what were they doing?” After Ch18 ask “What, specifically, did Pathwell fail to do? Would it necessarily have worked?” Log spontaneous answers and confidence; if they cannot name any plausible act, test *one* extra responder cue against the unchanged control. Avoid priming “there was already one man.”

## Decision
**TABLETOP done; FOCUS FOR BLIND TEST.** No manuscript change, author-level choice, promotion or L4 sign-off. Retain both Shade's lack of consent and Pathwell's shame.
