## Hypothesis
- hyp: The fire, cleanup entry, Shade realization and Elizabeth's rescue can share one event window with clear agency and no new magical shortcut.

## Test
- test: Compare existing Chapter 15 with two **noncanonical beat sheets**, without writing prose.

## Scope / limits
- scope: Rung Q28–Q54, Chapter 7, Chapters 12–15, World Bible and Decisions. Only planning documents may change.
- limits: No new magical skill, no added children by fiat, no causal blame reassigned to Shade, no unannounced POV shift, no assumption Q54 is decided.

## Baseline — Current Chapter 14–15
1. Pathwell's failed rejoining causes physical fire.
2. Milo is trapped; Elizabeth enters archive, bypasses diary/cookbook, frees Milo.
3. Stansbury performs salvage and is injured; Elizabeth returns outside.
4. Blob appears; Shade voluntarily walks away to attract it and accepts loss.
**Existing failure:** Elizabeth's rescue does not materially change the blob encounter. The creature's access to Camp is not justified on the page.

## Candidate A — Caught, then becomes still
1. A pursuing blob nears Camp's boundary before the failed working; one observer might glimpse resistance.
2. Pathwell's working fails, spillage acts as a beacon and the lantern ignites the archive.
3. The blob compresses across Camp's boundary during the emergency; a child tries to warn someone.
4. Milo is trapped; Elizabeth and Shade see the fire, and Shade begins moving to help.
5. Shade notices the creature is already at his shin. Elizabeth recognizes his peril while hearing Milo; she heads inside.
6. Shade becomes still as the creature overtakes him, facing the cost of his fixation; he does not demand anything from Pathwell.
**Strength:** Quiet reflection. **Blocker:** Stillness may read as paralysis; if Elizabeth is inside, her POV cannot witness his private resolution.

## Candidate B — Caught, still tries to reach Camp
1–5. Same chronology as Candidate A.
6. After he notices the creature, Shade makes an observable attempt toward people needing help, then recognizes how much of his brief opportunity has been lost; subsequent resistance/acceptance remains to be decided.
**Strength:** Active compassion is visually legible. **Blocker:** If he is trapped, his movement may not alter anything. Could look like a convenient late heroic redemption rather than tragic moral clarity.

## Counterfactual checks
- **Without the failed merge:** no new spillage beacon or archive ignition; existing Shade anomaly still attracts cleanup over time. This strongly joins magic and accident to Pathwell's choice.
- **Without Shade's demand for a public reckoning:** Pathwell might still try to force a rejoining; present facts do not prove Shade *caused* the fire. Do not make Shade culpable for Pathwell's coercive act.
- **Without Elizabeth rescuing Milo:** the child remains endangered; however, the blob's reason for coming and its true target are unchanged. This means the revised scene does not yet **prove** Elizabeth changes the central magical outcome.
- **With Elizabeth free to intervene:** she has a dagger previously used against a blob and would plausibly try. The new candidate must establish timely spatial constraints, not simply ask readers to forget the dagger.
- **Without a viewpoint change:** if Elizabeth goes inside, Shade's private thought and final decision are unseen. A reader may be given a clear earlier observable action, or a properly staged witness; do not head-hop.

## Result
- result: The experiment supports **testing a combined climax**, but fails to establish a finished, causally complete sequence. Candidate B offers clearer pre-capture agency; A may better preserve quiet tragedy. This is comparison only, **not a selection**. The largest blocking problems are simultaneity, Elizabeth's exact knowledge and available time, and POV. The later Q55 question about Pathwell witnessing is still open.

## Pass criteria before promotion
1. One beat-by-beat timeline with approximate action order and believable proximity.
2. Elizabeth's knowledge, choice and inability to conduct both rescues are visible without making her responsible for Shade's loss.
3. Shade's missed opportunity is legible without exonerating Pathwell or treating short-lived Shade as deserving punishment.
4. The creature's forced boundary crossing follows from prior pursuit and fresh spillage, not teleportation.
5. The limited viewpoint has an honest way to communicate Shade's final moment.
6. Explicit author approval reconciles older Q132/Decisions/Character Bible.
