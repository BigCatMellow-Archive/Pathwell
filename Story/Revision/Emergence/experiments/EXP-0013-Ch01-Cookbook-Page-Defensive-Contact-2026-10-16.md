**Date:** 2026-10-16

## Controlling evidence

1. The sugar-cookie recipe is **already committed** when Elizabeth pulls the cookbook away, leaving the page under Pathwell's palm. An interrupted working continues, spills loose gold, and the blob cleans that fresh casting waste. Author August interview §2 (Interrupted cookbook working) and active world bible make this explicit.
2. The page's already-established work is **protective**. The earlier wiki `Magic-System.md` gives “golden protective light” for the cookbook, but it is archived background, not an independent live authority. The active Ch1 physically reports a bright collision and recoil. Do not infer a generic projectile attack or a new spell slot.
3. Elizabeth is an ordinary observer: this is a physical collision and light she can see, not special perception.
4. The blob is **already at Elizabeth's front door before the spell** because it followed the ambiguous Pathwell/Shade signature. The strike does not cause its arrival. After the blob removes the local waste, it still pursues the active glowing page and only later is dispersed by the remainder of the working.
5. The original “where it was met sharply by a blinding strike” has a blurry grammatical subject, while the revised action can be rooted in the **one source already visible to the reader**.

**Choice for this revision pass:** EXP-0013 B, because it is the only one that makes the action readable **without inventing an independent casting ability**. It does not establish whether all emotionally charged pages act as barriers. The observed effect belongs to this specific already-active cookbook working. The next standalone sentence, “The impact sang.”, remains exactly as before, letting the author’s concise sound carry the collision.
