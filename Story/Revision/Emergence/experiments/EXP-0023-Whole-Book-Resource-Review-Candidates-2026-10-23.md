## The headline

**The book holds together.** All ten Non-Negotiable Scenes are on the page and nine land. Who holds what tracks across all 18 chapters, and the magic rules don't break. What the reviewers agree on is the **back half explaining itself**:
- Chapters 1–7 use *choose/choice/decide* 0–3 times each, and Chapters 8–18 use them 4–10 times each (targets reviewer);
- the narrator labels Elizabeth's choices as choices, and Ch16–17 restate what Ch14–15 showed (all three reviewers);
- from Ch6 on, one corrective deadpan runs through the cast (aim reviewer; EXP-0021 covers part of it).

The author's own voice guide names this: "every emotional scene explaining what the reader should feel"; "Growth should appear in changed behavior before it appears in speeches"; "question → character choice → consequence, over question → speech explaining the theme". Almost every fix is a cut.

**Protect** (aim reviewer, section 1): Nana's soup note (Ch4), FIND NANA'S SPOON (Ch12), the coffee laugh that breaks into crying (Ch16), Milo's boot (Ch16), LIKED POCKETS (Ch16), SAM HOME TODAY (Ch11), the author's own rough jokes in Ch10–11, the Merritt margins and the geese (Ch18).

## D. For the author's pen (no patch: each needs new text)

1. **Ch14:441**, Shade: "You still think the result gets to decide whether the choice was acceptable." It's nearly the Thesis page's Argument word for word. Cutting it leaves Pathwell two lines in a row (aim, targets).
2. **The cookbook's second life at Camp** (Non-Negotiable 3). Ch4 promises use, and Ch12 shows only a card and narrates "The book belonged here now" (Ch12:317). One trace of use inside an existing Ch12 or Ch13 beat (targets).
3. **The prune's body cost after Ch14.** His hand shakes after the Ch3 prune (Ch5, 6, 7). After the larger Ch14 prune there's only "Pruning shock." (Ch16:176). One staging line, for example at Ch16:174, would keep R30's cost visible and keep Ch18's "can he still?" alive (targets).
4. **The healed young woman** (Ch4) disappears after "gone from the cot" (Ch12:60), and the Promise Ledger has no row for her. A line, or a ledger row marking her open on purpose (world).
5. **Mama Baga's aphorisms in Ch16** (508, 576): "Then stop naming impossible payment as if saying a large enough number is accountability." By Ch16 she lectures; R33 has her "dry in speech and warm in action" (aim).
6. **Where the prose should stretch.** In Ch12–18, 26–35% of narration paragraphs are six words or fewer, and only 2–4% of sentences reach 25 words; his Ch4–7 drafts have 36–40% at that length. The reviewer suggests Ch16's grief night, Ch17's run of "still"s at 400, and Ch18's browsing (185–209). His voice guide: "Reflection and emotion: allow thoughts to stretch" (aim).
7. **Voices drifting in the back half:** Pathwell into the house's clipped formal register from Ch10 ("I am developing standards."); Elizabeth from snapping to "the book's articulate auditor"; Stansbury mostly correcting after Ch5 (EXP-0021 covers part). The reviewer counts 15 "That's not what I asked / not the same thing" lines across 8 speakers; Ch14:129 and Ch11:538 are the easiest cuts. "Cruel." appears three times as Pathwell's comeback, and "Important distinction." twice (aim).
8. **Ch16:726**, "or make the diary original again": the diary was never copied. Cut the clause, or he knows what he meant (world).
