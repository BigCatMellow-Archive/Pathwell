# Three versions, in pieces: forks and harvest (2026-11-17)

## Status

**A map for bt 7274 to pick from. Nothing is decided and nothing was written.** It lays the [manuscript](../../Chapters/), the [Garden](../Experiments/Garden/) and the [Wild Garden](../Experiments/Wild/) side by side, story stretch by story stretch, and sorts what's in them into three kinds:

- **Forks:** places where the versions made different story choices and only one can win.
- **Harvest:** pieces from the Garden or the Wild Garden that could move into the manuscript.
- **What stays home:** pieces that don't travel, and why.

Built from the three evaluations ([manuscript](MANUSCRIPT-EVALUATION-2026-11-17.md), [Garden](../Experiments/Garden/Evaluation-2026-11-16.md), [Wild](../Experiments/Wild/Evaluation-2026-11-16.md)) and the chapters they quote. It follows the R67 standing rule: tradeoffs shown, no "Recommended," and my leans labeled as leans.

**Ground rules this map keeps:**

- **The spine is the manuscript.** It's the version built on bt 7274's own voice, and its foundation is the soundest of the three.
- **Ch1 is bt 7274's own text** (restored by R67). Anything here that would touch Ch1 or Ch2 is flagged, and is his call alone.
- **Ruled things stay ruled.** That covers the climax events (the [2026-10-15 ruling](../Author-Ruling-Ch14-Ch15-Climax-2026-10-15.md)), "someone" (R54), the confession in Ch16 (R66) and Ch15's line 21 (R67). Where a fork touches one, it says so.
- **New lore needs him** (R23). A piece that adds a fact the book doesn't have is marked **lore**.
- **Lines are his.** A piece that needs a new joke, emotional line or document text is marked **your words**. This map names the place and the job, never the words.
- **R58's order stands.** This page is for beside the paper read, not instead of it.

**How to use it:** mark each fork with a choice, and each harvest row **yours**, **no**, or **later**. Picks go on to EXP pages as candidates, revised toward the voice of Ch1–6.

## The lean, in one paragraph

**Lean:** keep the manuscript whole, and take about seven pieces. From the Garden: half of the owing thread, the cake's route, and her own recipe card shown. From the Wild Garden: one document in the voice of the person first wronged, a face-to-face reckoning (carried by Milo), "Drive" as a caregiver's code, and the long-paragraph rhythm of its Elizabeth-alone chapter. Most of the forks go the manuscript's way. One is a real question for bt 7274 before Ch14–15 are edited: the Sunday promise.

Under this lean, Ch1–6 aren't touched. The changes land in Ch7, 9, 10 or 17, 12, 15–16, 17 and 18. None of the three versions supplies the voice fix (the manuscript evaluation's P1 and P2): all three share the house deadpan. The cure for that is in bt 7274's own Ch1–6.

## Forks

Each fork is one choice. The options are what the three versions did, plus any middle path the pieces allow.

### K1. Does the first person wronged get a voice and an account?

- **Manuscript:** Ada Kell and Tess, told only in Pathwell's voice (Ch10), named once more (Ch14), then dropped. bt 7274 marked them his (R67).
- **Garden:** the same, plus an unrelated Ada Lowe quilt that muddies it.
- **Wild Garden:** Wren, dead, speaking for herself in a letter. Her letter is the best prose in that book.
- **Middle path:** keep Ada and Tess, and give one of them one document in her own voice (**H4**), or one line that keeps their account open.
- **Sunday and tone check:** the Framework wants "interested in people rather than suffering." A wronged person who speaks once is more people, not more suffering.
- **Cost:** H4 is **lore** and **your words**.
- **Lean:** keep Ada and Tess, and give one of them a voice. It closes the manuscript's F1 at its root.
- Note: the name Wren can't travel. The manuscript's Wren is the girl at the stream (Ch13).

### K2. How does Shade's story end?

- **Manuscript and Garden:** he dies at Camp, taken while Elizabeth is inside (ruled).
- **Wild Garden:** he lives, but the cut takes what he inherited, and he forgets everyone. The rule it states doesn't match what happens (Wild F4).
- **Sunday and tone check:** the Framework leans toward Wild ("the characters will probably be okay"). The book's weight leans toward death: it's caused, it costs for three chapters, and it isn't "solely to prove seriousness."
- **Lean:** death, as ruled. The Wild Garden's ending is moving, but it brings a rule problem the manuscript doesn't have.

### K3. Does the Sunday promise hold in Ch14–15?

- **Manuscript and Garden:** no, by events. An eleven-year-old is under a shelf in a fire while a man is eaten in the yard.
- **Wild Garden:** yes, by manner. "Afterward" openers (Ch8, Ch10) tell the reader in advance that everyone came through.
- **Options:**
  - (a) Rule that the promise is about manner, and leave the events as they are.
  - (b) Borrow one Wild-style opener (**H8**), so the reader knows Milo lives.
  - (c) Shorten Milo's time in danger.
- **Cost:** (b) and (c) trade suspense for the promise. Neither changes a ruled event.
- **No strong lean.** It's the one ruling worth making before anyone edits Ch14–15.

### K4. Who records Shade?

- **Manuscript:** Camp does. The archivist writes SHADE — DIED AT CAMP. and LIKED POCKETS, and files it.
- **Garden:** he does, in his own notebook ("Ate a pear. Didn't like it… That one's mine, then."), and it survives him.
- These don't stack. If he writes himself down, the archivist's line loses its reason to exist.
- **Lean (weak):** Camp. LIKED POCKETS is the manuscript's funeral, and it shows a community deciding he was a person. The cost: the Garden's notebook entries are the best writing in that version, and only a notebook gives Shade his own written voice.

### K5. Is owing a thread?

- **Manuscript:** half-built and unnamed. Shade pays for her breakfast (Ch9). "He still owes me a hand" (Milo, Ch16). "Your account remains at zero" (Ch18).
- **Garden:** a full chain. $2.65 (Ch2) → coins counted back (Ch6) → "Elizabeth owes me breakfast" → "Owe Shade breakfast" → "Paid." → the unpaid line burning in her diary → THANK YOU.
- **Options:**
  - None: leave it as is.
  - Half (**H1**): name the breakfast debt between Ch9 and Ch12, so it burns unpaid with the diary in Ch15.
  - Full: start it in Ch2 with the coins.
- **Cost:** the full chain touches Ch2, which is his text, and raises the book's payoff density. "Everything pays off" is the AI-sounding feature all three evaluations name.
- **Lean:** half. It uses seams the manuscript already has, and leaves Ch2 alone.

### K6. What's Elizabeth's habit?

- **Manuscript:** caregiving control (the applesauce, the plan for Shade) and carrying (the thirteen stairs).
- **Garden:** hospitality (the cab and water for an intruder, the tray, the cake).
- **Wild Garden:** "sorry," counted.
- **Lean:** the manuscript. It's the only one where her flaw is the same as Pathwell's, which is what makes Ch18 work. The Garden's hospitality is already in the manuscript as carrying. The Wild Garden's sorry would make her a different woman.

### K7. Does anyone wronged sit across from Pathwell and set terms?

- **Manuscript:** institutions (the curator, the archivist, Mama Baga's chair) and Elizabeth. Milo nearly does it, through cards.
- **Wild Garden:** Kevin on the rug: "You don't know. You weren't there." / "No. You're right. I wasn't." Then BY PATHWELL.
- **Middle path:** Milo, who was trapped by a fire Pathwell caused and lost a boot to it, sets one term of his own (**H5**).
- **Lean:** yes, through Milo. It's small, and it's the one thing the Wild Garden's restitution does that the manuscript's doesn't.

### K8. Her crash?

- **Manuscript:** the ditch, with "Then we stop," a checked road, "Seatbelt.", brakes and an apology, and the farmer paid later.
- **Wild Garden:** the pond, with none of that.
- **Lean:** the manuscript, which passes C4.

## What stays home, and why

- **The Wild Garden's sorry habit.** It's that book's Elizabeth, not this one's (K6).
- **Kevin's bear as the override.** It would trade the museum for a library and lose the quarantine letter, Henry Vale and SAM HOME TODAY. The function travels through Milo (H5). The bear doesn't.
- **The Wild Garden's pond.** It fails C4 (K8).
- **The cut and the forgetting.** Only through K2, and its rule would need fixing.
- **The free cart for asked help** (Wild Ch18). It dissolves the shop's resistance, which "A month is not a character trait" keeps.
- **"I picked you. For that."** (Wild Ch18). The manuscript's reason (he'd "already decided what the danger was") is planted on page two and paid in Ch10. The Wild Garden's is answered before anyone asks it.
- **The Ada Lowe quilt** (Garden Ch17). It muddies K1.
- **The Garden's extra maxims and proofs.** They're the manuscript's P2 and P3, only more of them.
- **Both experiments' country drift.** The Wild Garden is set in England; the Garden turns British at Ch10. The manuscript is American.
- **The name Wren** (K1).

## What fits together, and what fights

- **H1, H3 and Milo's "He still owes me a hand"** make one quiet thread with no new scene: debts written down, burned, owed to the dead, and one recipe written fresh.
- **H4 and H5** together answer the manuscript's F1 and the Wild Garden's best scene at once. Someone wronged speaks, and someone wronged sets terms. They could be two people, or the same document could do both.
- **H3 and the geese ending:** the card in her pocket at the door joins the Ready / No / Perfect reversal without replacing anything.
- **H1 against payoff density:** each piece that pays off makes the book tidier, and tidiness is the shared AI tell. If H1, H2 and H3 all go in, something else tidy could come out. Ch17's passed tests (manuscript F5) are the first place to look.
- **H10 against LIKED POCKETS:** pick one (K4).
- **H8 against suspense:** it only makes sense if K3 is ruled that way.
