# Insight Record — INS-0005: Loss, custody and non-replacement

Project: Pathwell  
Related task: NONE  
Detected by: Current 18-chapter sweep, 2026-10-16  
Status: RAW / NOT PROMOTED  
Scope: Ch1, 3–4, 11–12, 15–18

## Observation
The book does not simply oppose magical barter to sentimental attachment. It distinguishes **ownership, consent to use, copying, custody and restoration**: five practices that Pathwell initially treats as close substitutes, and other characters insist are different.

## Evidence
- [Ch1](../../../Chapters/Chapter_01.txt): Pathwell takes Nana's personal records.
- [Ch3](../../../Chapters/Chapter_03.txt): “These were never his to spend” sets a property boundary while he pays with his own possibility.
- [Ch4](../../../Chapters/Chapter_04.txt): Elizabeth gives a whole cookbook to save a woman; the rest is archived with provenance.
- [Ch11](../../../Chapters/Chapter_11.txt): scans cannot restore lost Daniel Vale originals.
- [Ch12](../../../Chapters/Chapter_12.txt): giving a diary does not waive donor reading restrictions.
- [Ch15](../../../Chapters/Chapter_15.txt): donated originals still matter profoundly when they burn.
- [Ch17](../../../Chapters/Chapter_17.txt): Pathwell offers another Vale-family letter as a possible replacement; curator says no, but separate donation is possible.
- [Ch18](../../../Chapters/Chapter_18.txt): the Merritt cookbook may be valuable without being an acceptable replacement for Nana's.

## Synthesis
Loss isn't measured solely by information or utility; its moral seriousness grows from **who could authorize its use and whose lived object it was**. This is why the marked exhibit case remains empty and why copied routes still have practical value.

## Risk
Do not imply that copies are worthless or that custodians should never use records. The archivist legitimately makes route copies, the healer legitimately spends an authorized page, and survivors need restoration of function.

## Smallest safe test
[Compare](../synthesis/SYN-0004-custody-without-restoration.md) Ch4 donation, Ch12 donor card, Ch17 exhibition and Ch18 put-back for conflicting implicit rules. Ask whether readers understand the difference *without* a narrator's explanation.

## Disposition
KEEP as a lens and protect in editing; no additional object backstory or recovery magic justified.
