# Insight Record — INS-0007: Shade grows a life he didn't inherit

Project: Pathwell  
Related task: NONE  
Detected by: Whole-book sweep, 2026-10-16  
Status: RAW / NOT PROMOTED  
Scope: Ch8–9, 12–17

## Observation
Shade has partial historical memory inherited from Pathwell, but his strongest evidence of individuality is the **new behavior that experience teaches him**. He doesn't remember playing Hearts; he learns people at the table. He changes clothes, preferences and intentions while his hand still feels the imposed draw.

## Evidence
- [Ch8](../../../Chapters/Chapter_08.txt): Shade names himself, resists his hand, and doesn't recognize Elizabeth.
- [Ch9](../../../Chapters/Chapter_09.txt): Prague and Celia are remembered facts, not lived relationships; he corrects “we” to “he.”
- [Ch12](../../../Chapters/Chapter_12.txt): he respects consent at Elizabeth's treatment; Camp asks him where he hurts.
- [Ch13](../../../Chapters/Chapter_13.txt): green jacket, bad coffee he chooses to like, Hearts, pockets, onions, “I want to stay here.”
- [Ch14](../../../Chapters/Chapter_14.txt): “Do I remain myself?” has a concrete present tense; he refuses rejoining.
- [Ch15](../../../Chapters/Chapter_15.txt): he begins to help with the fire before the creature catches him; he fights to live and calls for help.
- [Ch16](../../../Chapters/Chapter_16.txt): Camp records his name, his green jacket, and “LIKED POCKETS.” [Ch17](../../../Chapters/Chapter_17.txt): Hearts goes on with a different fourth player, not a resurrected substitute.

## Emerging possibility
Death is a violent interruption of a **possible life**, not a character test he voluntarily passes. The absence of a reported internal final “regret” may protect the POV and dignity of a dying person; a precise last insight is not necessary to prove his humanity.

## Risk
Do not retroactively describe his origin as wholly irrelevant: the draw, memories and fixation are real constraints. Do not make him nobler by giving up the struggle or more culpable for Pathwell's actions.

## Test
In an uninformed reading, ask: “What had Shade begun doing that he could not have done by simply copying Pathwell?” If readers cite Hearts and the Camp request rather than only the climax, the human cost is on the page. A weak result does **not** automatically license inserted Camp days (same-morning timeline).

## Disposition
KEEP. Replaces no existing INS-0001; complements that card by emphasizing **observable life** over inferred final introspection.
