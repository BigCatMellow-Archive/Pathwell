# Decisions: Pathwell revision

## Status

**Active. Book record, created 2026-09-29 in [pass P1](Pass-Log.md).** This page owns James's rulings for this revision of the Pathwell manuscript, the earlier rulings that bear on it, the working decisions made in the work, and the questions waiting on him. It follows the [per-collection decisions](../Sunday-Morning/Decisions.md#per-collection-decisions) shape.

- **James's rulings bind.** Build on them; only James changes one. Record a change here with its date, then update the page it applies to.
- **Recorded rulings** are author decisions that earlier sessions wrote down. They're quoted from the record named in each row, which is where their exact wording and history live. Where the manuscript doesn't yet reflect one, it's also a question in [Open for James](#open-for-james), because the record may belong to an architecture he set aside.
- **Working decisions** were made in the work and are reversible until James reads them.

The standing rulings of the Sunday Morning method that are about *method* carry over under R1: D1 (MAPS_L with THINK and PLAN), D6 (the voice guide is a sensibility, not a checklist), D12 (change only what genuinely improves the text) and D14 (write how people talk, in narration too). Its rulings about Sunday Morning tone, sagas and collections (D2, D8's collection scope, D9, D15, D16) don't apply to this book.

## Open for James

The verdicts, canon and taste calls this revision can't settle alone. Each says what it blocks and what I'd recommend, with the strongest case against where there is one.

**Q1. Is Coda.txt still part of the book?** Your request lists it as canon; the repository says it was superseded. The Aug 28 reconciliation wrote Chapter 18 as "the coda" (commit message "Write final coda with restraint and Elizabeth-led adventure"; [execution log](../Story_Files/MANUSCRIPT_RECONCILIATION_EXECUTION_LOG_CONTINUED_13_2026-08-28.md): "The coda is a lean new Chapter Eighteen"), [Story/README.md](../README.md) and `assemble_manuscript.py` stop at Chapter 18, and the Coda's own contents are the ones the [WC contract](../Story_Files/PATHWELL_MAPS_L_CHAPTER_CONTRACT_MATRIX_2026-08-28.md#wc--coda--restraint--elizabeth-initiates) lists as stale ("pruning simply failing; charged receipt fuel; Shopkeeper assigning final quest"). It also contradicts the book: *The Joy of Cooking* for Nana's handwritten book, "given away" when it burned, a windowsill in a shop Ch18 says has no windows. The cold reader said it "reads as if it belongs to a different draft" and made them doubt Ch18. *Recommendation:* retire it (move it out of `Chapters/`; git keeps it). *Case against:* its one good idea is Pathwell's relief when a prune doesn't happen, and that idea may be what your 2026-09-18 ruling wants in Ch18 (Q2). *Blocks:* ending work.

**Q2. How does the ending go?** Two recorded 2026-09-18 rulings don't match Ch18 as written: (a) Pathwell silently puts the other family's cookbook back without asking a price, starting a transaction or learning whether he can still prune; (b) the last lines are `"Ready?" Elizabeth asked.` / `"No," Pathwell said.` / `"Good."`. Ch18 instead stages the full near-prune at the ledger (the branches, "Still capable of division") and ends on "Perfect.", with the attribution reading as Pathwell asking. *Case for Ch18 as written:* the cold reader called the ledger refusal "the book's real ending for Pathwell", a mirror of Ch3's payment. *Case for the ruling:* the explicit test answers the one question you wanted left open, and the near-prune turns his restraint into a demonstration. *Either way:* the final exchange's attribution needs fixing so Elizabeth unmistakably asks, and the stray `}` after the last word goes. *Blocks:* Ch18 above line level.

**Q3. Does the opening change?** Three recorded rulings touch Chapters 1 and 2, which a July note locked: the welcome-party opening (A2), no lo-mein agency rung (A5), and "It was the crash that woke her." repeating at the midpoint (A1). The welcome party answers the cold reader's first and biggest unanswered question (why Pathwell was in *her* apartment) and the "Lizzy" puzzle (he'd have heard her name at her own party). *Recommendation:* apply A2 as a light revision of Ch1 that keeps the benchmark sugar-cookie scene word for word; decide A5 by whether you still want the lo mein and spoon rhyme (Ch2 → Ch17), which currently reads as an agency beat; and place the A1 echo yourself, since it's a final-rhythm line. *Case against:* Ch1–2 are your benchmark chapters and the only ones that already sound like you. *Blocks:* Ch1, Ch2, Ch8's midpoint line.

**Q4. Must Elizabeth's climax choice change the central conflict?** Your 2026-09-18 correction rule says it must. As written, her choice (Milo over the diary and cookbook) is the thesis in action (Thesis page: "Elizabeth matters without any power source") but the Pathwell–Shade outcome would run the same without it. If the rule applies, it's a book-level problem and goes to THINK (options in the [plan](Plan.md#think-at-book-level)); if the Thesis page's test is the one that governs, the climax meets it now. *Blocks:* Ch14–15 above line level.

**Q5. Which version of the pillars governs?** Non-Negotiable-Scenes.md, which you named as an authority, predates the reconciliation and disagrees with canon.md and the manuscript on six of its ten scenes: #2 (Elizabeth cuts *herself* out; now she cuts Pathwell out, ruling C1), #3 (the cookbook heals Mama Baga; now a young woman), #4 (the crash is unintentional; now deliberate), #7 (an "anger injection"), #9 (eye contact stops the draw; the WC-era contract calls that stale) and #10 (the private retry fails quietly; superseded by the 2026-09-18 ruling). The Thesis page's rows for Shade ("the Lie made manifest") and Stansbury ("morally questionable path") also predate Writing Principles 15 and ruling E3. *Recommendation:* treat canon.md plus the recorded rulings as the current pillars and let me bring Non-Negotiable-Scenes.md into line once you confirm. *Blocks:* nothing in Step 2; it governs later passes.

**Q6. When does Elizabeth know the man is Shade?** Ruling C4 says at the diner; the manuscript has Shade say his name on the road. The road version is clearer and the cold reader followed it easily; the diner version keeps a mystery going for one more chapter. *Recommendation:* keep the manuscript and retire C4. *Blocks:* Ch8–9 above line level.

**Q7. Deliberate crash or deliberate refusal?** Open in the [disagreement register](../Story_Files/PATHWELL_AUTHOR_EDITORIAL_DISAGREEMENT_REGISTER_2026-09-18.md#disagreement-3--midpoint-crash-deliberate-crash-vs-deliberate-refusal-with-unintended-collision). The manuscript has her choose the ditch, with seatbelts called first; the cold reader called it "a delicious escalation". *Recommendation:* keep it deliberate and close the disagreement.

**Q8. Who asked Pathwell for help?** The prune that made Shade is answered morally in Ch10 and left blank in fact ("exact event that created Shade" is an open question in the [recovered decisions](../Story_Files/PATHWELL_RECOVERED_DECISIONS_AUDIT_2026-09-17.md#13-genuine-open-questions-after-recovery)). After four chapters of "Which prune?" the cold reader felt "half persuaded, half cheated". *Options:* name the problem in a line of Ch10 without explaining the magic, or keep it blank and give the withholding a human reason on the page (Writing Principles 10). *Blocks:* Ch10 above line level.

**Q9. Smaller calls.** (a) The healer is unnamed though her "Can I touch it?" carries the theme; name her? (b) The foam dagger: I'd have it considered and set aside at the climax, or lost on the page at the museum, rather than silently gone ([ledger O4](Promise-Ledger.md#objects)); tell me if you'd rather it stay out of the climax entirely. (c) Which chapter is the Step 2 test ([plan](Plan.md#step-2-the-test-chapter)).
