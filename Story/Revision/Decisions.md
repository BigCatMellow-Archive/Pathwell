# Decisions: Pathwell revision

## Status

**Active. Book record, created 2026-09-29 in [pass P1](Pass-Log.md).** This page owns the author's rulings for this revision of the Pathwell manuscript, the earlier rulings that bear on it, the working decisions made in the work, and the questions waiting on him. It follows the [per-collection decisions](../Sunday-Morning/Decisions.md#per-collection-decisions) shape.

- **The author's rulings bind.** Build on them; only the author changes one. Record a change here with its date, then update the page it applies to.
- **The Bible interview locks** are the author's answers to the one-question-at-a-time interview of 2026-08-21 to 08-27 (the Bible decisions and audit questions Q1–Q149). They're the primary record of his earlier rulings and bind like his rulings here ([W8](#working-decisions)). Each is summarized with a link to its record, where the exact wording lives.
- **Later recorded rulings** (2026-09-17/18) were written down during the Experimental Architecture V3–V5 work, which was never applied to the chapters. Where one agrees with the interview it confirms it; where it differs, it's a question in [Open for the author](#open-for-the-author).
- **Working decisions** were made in the work and are reversible until the author reads them.

The standing rulings of the Sunday Morning method that are about *method* carry over under R1: D1 (MAPS_L with THINK and PLAN), D6 (the voice guide is a sensibility, not a checklist), D12 (change only what genuinely improves the text) and D14 (write how people talk, in narration too). Its rulings about Sunday Morning tone, sagas and collections (D2, D8's collection scope, D9, D15, D16) don't apply to this book.
