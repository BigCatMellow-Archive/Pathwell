3. **Reference audit.** Two readers audited the reference pages: canon, the world and character bibles, the glossary, the contract matrix, the voice notes, the three root pages, the ledger and the plan.
4. **Verification.** A separate reader checked the 18 most consequential findings against the primary record: 12 hold, 5 partly hold, and none fails ([verification](Currency-Audit-2026-10-01/verification.md)).

The full findings, with line numbers and sources, are in [Currency-Audit-2026-10-01/](Currency-Audit-2026-10-01/). Each reader's file also lists the locks it checked that the text already follows.

## The short version

- **The chapters are mostly current.** Every chapter follows most of its locks; the readers counted 9 to 22 followed per chapter.
- **There are 113 findings across the chapters,** about a third of them already known to the [plan](Plan.md#chapter-work-lists). Most are scene-level staging that the 2026-08-28 rewrite didn't carry over from the interview: who is in the room, what is said aloud and in front of whom, which lines were locked to be kept. A few are story-level.
- **There are about 60 findings across the reference pages,** mostly stale lines that later locks replaced. Chief among them: Stansbury's flaw as it was before Q128, the glossary's [OPEN] markers on settled things, and Shade's survival "blocked".
- **Four places where the author's own decisions disagree with each other.** These need his ruling, not a fix; they are listed first below.

## For the author: where your decisions disagree with each other

1. **Chapter 18's prune: Q96 or Q140?**
   - **Q96 (08-26):** Pathwell "chooses **not to find out**", which "supersedes older coda-support language requiring familiar pruning signs to visibly prove that the ability still works".
   - **Q140 (08-27, a lock about point of view):** describes the passage as "where Pathwell privately begins a prune, confirms that he still can, and chooses to stop before release". Q138 also says he "remains fully capable… chooses to stop before release".
   - **Where they meet:** all agree that he stops. They differ on whether his ability is *confirmed*.
   - **A version that satisfies both:** he feels the prune begin to take, so he knows privately that he still can, and he stops before it would settle or go wrong.
   - **Which pages follow which:** the revision's Decisions, Plan and ledger follow Q96. The contract matrix, canon, world bible and Non-Negotiable Scenes each follow a different version ([verification 18](Currency-Audit-2026-10-01/verification.md)).
2. **Chapter 18's next errand: found in the shop, or chosen before she arrives?**
   - **Q97 and Q98:** the shopkeeper presents an errand and Elizabeth claims it.
   - **Q135 (newer):** she arrives "with the next direction/adventure **already chosen by her**".
   - **Q137:** reads Q135 as "decision ownership".
   - **The chapter now:** she picks the geese route inside the shop.
3. **"Lizzy": the banner or her grandmother?**
   - **The glossary:** "Nicknamed 'Lizzy' by her grandmother — Pathwell knows this".
   - **Chapter 1 since P7:** "WELCOME LIZZY" on the banner is where he got it (the [Q-H](Decisions.md#open-for-the-author) default).
   - Either can stand. If it was her grandmother's name for her, his using it would sting, and her correcting him to "Elizabeth" would carry more.
4. **The mirror in Chapter 6: the lock, or the scene as written?**
   - **The lock (08-23 §6):** "Pathwell sees Shade through the wrong-angle/reflected perspective". Q140 still names "the wrong-angle reflection".
   - **The chapter now:** shows only a vague wrongness, and he reasons his way to "Mine".
   - The assessment praised the scene, so keeping it would mean waiving the lock.
