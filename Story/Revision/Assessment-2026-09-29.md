## Summary

The book works. The cold reader was gripped by every set piece built from the spent-record magic, by Shade, and by the book's refusal to cheat (the cookbook and diary burn for nothing while Elizabeth saves a child; Shade stays dead; Stansbury keeps his scar). The contracts are met almost everywhere. Four kinds of problem stand out, in this order of cost:

1. **The ending is doubled, and Chapter 18 drifted from the interview's locks** (Q94–Q103: a silent cookbook moment, and Pathwell choosing not to find out whether he can still prune). Book level; the staging is settled ([Decisions](Decisions.md#the-bible-interview-locks-the-manuscript-doesnt-yet-follow)), and only the last word is open ([Q-A](Decisions.md#open-for-james)).
2. **The opening's premise is never answered** (why Pathwell was in her apartment; how he knows "Lizzy"). Book level; the interview answers it (he passed as a guest at a party; [Decisions](Decisions.md#the-bible-interview-locks-the-manuscript-doesnt-yet-follow)), and whose party is [Q-B](Decisions.md#open-for-james).
3. **The narration keeps telling the reader what the scene just showed, in one cadence.** 240 short commentary paragraphs, 238 "Not…"/"No…" fragment paragraphs and 246 uncontracted forms in narration. Chapters 1–3 have 2–6 commentary paragraphs each; Chapters 7–18 have 10–30. Line level, but everywhere, and most of it in the cautious categories.
4. **The back third is slower than its material** (Ch13's montage; Ch16–17's run of "Pathwell is corrected and complies"). Chapter/scene level.

Every chapter is at **L2** after this pass: it has a plan (its contract), ledger rows and reconsideration triggers ([plan](Plan.md#reconsideration-triggers)). None is at L3 under this method, because the prose was written on 2026-08-28, before the [voice guide](../Sunday-Morning/Sources/Voice-Guide.md) arrived (2026-09-27), and hasn't been checked against it. The target for every chapter is **L4**.
