# Author ruling — Elizabeth's agency at the Camp climax

**Date:** 2026-10-15
**Status:** AUTHOR-SELECTED DIRECTION FOR ELIZABETH / full climax design still unapproved
**Source:** Direct author response to [EXP-0002](Emergence/experiments/EXP-0002-Elizabeth-Central-Agency-Counterfactual-2026-10-15.md): **“then lets go with the restrained approach”**.
**Older authority addressed:** [September 18 author-intent discussion](../Archive/Experiments-2026-09/PATHWELL_CURRENT_AUTHOR_INTENT_SHADE_CONSEQUENCE_NOTES_2026-09-18.md) §3; prior [revision Decisions Q-E](Decisions.md#open-for-the-author), [Plan options 1/2/3/3b](Plan.md#the-climax-what-the-story-has-already-planted).

## Chosen interpretation

**The restrained, human-centered approach (EXP-0002 Track H) is selected.** Elizabeth remains the novel's primary changing life. Her decisive act in the fire is choosing to save Milo, despite risking her already injured shoulder and giving up the chance to retrieve her diary and Nana's cookbook. She is not required to control the blob, magically prevent Shade's loss, cut the rejoining frame, or choose Shade's fate to be central to this story. In the *proposed* simultaneous-emergency staging, she may recognize Shade's danger but cannot undertake both rescues.

The stricter September 18 test — **her final major choice must alter the specific Pathwell–Shade magical outcome** — is **not a mandatory acceptance criterion under this ruling**. It is acknowledged as an earlier concern, not erased from history. Her choice still has material consequences: Milo's actual rescue, permanent losses, and the later direction of her own life. The novel's thesis remains “Elizabeth matters without magical power,” not “Elizabeth solves the central spell.”

## What this decision does NOT decide

- It does **not** approve the whole alternative Chapter 14–15 sequence; the proposed overlap of the fire and blob remains subject to the spatial, rescue-window and POV tests.
- It does **not** by itself supersede the older Q132/character-bible statement that Shade voluntarily approaches the blob or change Chapter 15. Subsequent Rung Q43–Q54 author answers point to **surprise capture** as a developing alternative, but full promotion has not occurred.
- It does **not** answer Q54 (does Shade continue to help or become still once caught) or Q55 (does Pathwell witness his last moments).
- It does **not** settle Camp's boundary mechanism, exact targeting and cleanup timing, or anyone's availability to rescue Shade.
- It does **not** authorize Elizabeth to perform magic, change Milo's rescue, restore lost artifacts, absolve Pathwell of causing the fire, or turn Shade into a disposable lesson.

## Editorial consequence

Stop generating special dagger interventions or contrived shifts of the blob's target solely to satisfy the September 18 counterfactual. Evaluate new climax variants instead against these questions:

1. Does Elizabeth **make an informed, voluntary human choice**, with tangible cost and consequences?
2. Does the book keep her character arc central before and after the catastrophe?
3. Do Pathwell's actions and their consequences remain his responsibility?
4. Does Shade retain personhood, even if his private realization and death cannot be controlled by Elizabeth?
5. Is the overlap coherent on the page, without an unavailable rescue or unexplained magic?

**Status boundary:** This approves the **agency criterion**, not any new prose, supernatural rule, ending gesture, or conflict mechanism. A formal chapter-revision plan, creative review and author approval remain necessary before changing the manuscript.
