How it was made, and how far to trust it:

- **Six readers extracted it from the primary records**, each reading its files in full. Every row quotes the record's own words and names its source. **The source files remain the authority.** This page is an index to them: before acting on a row, read the section it names.

The September rows come from records that mix AI proposals with author choices; only items those records attribute to the author are included.
