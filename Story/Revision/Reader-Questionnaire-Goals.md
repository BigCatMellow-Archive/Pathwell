# Reader questionnaire: the goals key

## Status

**Active. The key to the [Reader questionnaire](Reader-Questionnaire.md). Do not show it to a reader before their answers are written.** For each group of questions it says what the author's goals predict a reader should find, if the book is working, and what answer would be a warning. The goals come from the author's own records, with links; nothing here is new. Created 2026-10-06 at the author's request ("lets see if we get the answers that align with our goals").

How to use it: read the reader's answers first, then this page. Mark each goal **aligned** (the reader found it unprompted, in their own words), **partly** (found it with a gap, or only when the question pointed at it), or **missed** (didn't find it, or found the opposite). An unprompted find is worth more than one the question led to.

## The goals, in the author's records

- **The controlling idea** ([canon](../Story_Files/canon.md#the-controlling-idea)): "Freedom is preserved when a person chooses to act despite uncertainty, because the act of choosing — not the outcome — is what makes you human." Elizabeth "matters because her choices are her own." Determinism is purchased: Pathwell narrowed himself by selling his future; Shade is foreclosed possibility given flesh; Elizabeth's future is unmortgaged.
- **Elizabeth's arc**: Pulled → Pushing → Choosing ([canon](../Story_Files/canon.md#the-three-rhythms-elizabeths-arc-in-simplest-form)); "compliance under pressure" is her starting flaw ([character bible](../Story_Files/character_bible.md)); "adaptation → preference → voluntary continuation → refusal → deliberate action → ownership of consequence → self-direction" (Decision-Timeline, SEP RNA11). She never does magic.
- **The agency ladder** ([character bible](../Story_Files/character_bible.md#the-agency-ladder)): "Each rung must be something Elizabeth does, not a door someone else opens for her."
  1. She follows because Pathwell still has the cookbook (Ch1–2).
  2. She reclaims what is hers, then chooses the portal (Ch3).
  3. She gives up the cookbook to heal a stranger at Camp (Ch4).
  4. She cuts Pathwell out of the blob with Stansbury's dagger (Ch7).
  5. She chooses the crash (Ch8).
  6. The Ask: she turns to the person who hurt her and demands the truth (Ch10).
  7. She gives the diary to the Camp archive (Ch12).
  8. In the fire she runs past the diary and the cookbook to save Milo (Ch15).
  9. "Are you ready?": she initiates what comes next (Ch18).
- **Pathwell's arc** ([character bible](../Story_Files/character_bible.md#pathwell)): "Convenience dressed as mercy." He solves other people's problems by spending his own future and making himself necessary. He forces the working past every no (Ch14), and the failure is his. Restitution on Camp's terms: he asks, accepts no, lets others define the need (Q133, Q134). At the end, restraint without a demonstration: he puts the other cookbook back and doesn't find out whether he can still prune (Q96, Q99, Q103, R19). "It was my fault" means Shade, unexplained (Q94, Q95).
- **The consequence statement** (the author, 2026-09-18 §10): "Pathwell must discover that responsibility does not imply omnipotence. Causing a problem does not guarantee that he can repair it, and understanding the harm does not make the harm reversible." Losses stay lost (Q133b, Q143b).
- **Shade** (Q131, Q132, Continued 29–30): a person, not a thematic instrument (09-18 §11). His last act is his own: he sees his pursuit making others pay and stops; an informed risk, not martyrdom for Pathwell, not guilt, not the draw compelling him. His existence is not a mistake. Blame for the archive, her injury and the forced working is Pathwell's.
- **Stansbury**: a brother-specific, relational turn ("Let it be."); he goes into the fire by his own choice and keeps a scar Pathwell can't undo.
- **Mama Baga and Camp**: Camp defines the terms; Mama Baga tells him to stop; "Person is the word I have for that."
- **The climax rule** (the author, 2026-09-18 §3): "if the choice is removed, the climax should unfold differently." Recorded in P40 as **not met** by the current text (her rescue shapes Shade's reason but doesn't change the outcome); put to the author.
- **Tone** ([Sunday Morning rules](../Sunday-Morning/Rules.md#tone-guardrails); R24, light touch): "You do not need to brace yourself"; one sad moment said once; somewhere soft to land; endings land warm. Craft: show, don't explain after showing; no narrator grading; characters sound different.
- **The anti-drift test** (09-18 §11): Elizabeth is the primary changing life; consequences stay real; world rules hold; Shade stays a person.

## What each question is listening for

| Questions | Aligned if the reader… | A warning if the reader… |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1–4 (the book) | names choice, agency or mattering on her own terms; something like "you are not what others decide you are" or "the choosing is what's yours" | describes it as Pathwell's story, a magic-system story, or a romance |
| 5 (start and end) | sees her move from accommodating, quiet compliance to choosing and leading | sees no change, or change caused by others |
| 6 (her decisions) | lists most of the nine rungs unprompted, as things she did, with costs | lists fewer than about five, or describes several as things done to her or opened by someone else |
