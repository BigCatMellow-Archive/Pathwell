## 0. What worked

- **Ch1, a soft landing that earns its place.** "He sat on the floor with a box open beside him, reading her diary like a magazine in a waiting room." makes the stranger likeable before the blob arrives, and "The cake, somehow, was fine." gives the reader somewhere to land right after the gore (1:11, 1:111). The call-and-answer end holds: "Ready?" / "No." / "Perfect."
- **Ch2, the turn.** The dumpling comedy runs up to the ticket in the gutter, and then "She heard her again. / Lizzy." changes the whole chapter in two lines (2:155-157). "He left the last dumpling on her side of the tray." (2:149) is the right size of kindness.
- **Ch3, cost with a cushion.** The fire and the million voices keep their weight (3:229-233), and the chapter then lets go at once: "Will that be paper or plastic?" / "Surprise me." (3:237-241), and "Don't make anything of that." (3:277).
- **Ch4, the wagon and the archive.** "Nobody looked at Elizabeth. Somehow that made the cookbook heavier." (4:206); the grease-marked "MORE IF THEY'RE SICK. THEY WON'T EAT ENOUGH." and "She believed soup was an argument you could win." (4:240-246); the HESS line in the road book (4:456), which shows what an archive is for; "Not hidden. Not displayed. Available." (4:446).
- **Ch5, Elizabeth acts.** "I'm the missus." with the elbow (5:133-139); "He lies, you know." paid off with "I don't lie." / "That settled it." (5:170, 5:311-313); "whether the cars would just migrate from one to the other" (5:301).
- **Ch6 hand-off.** "You came to a bar. I happen to be in it." (6:37) lands because Ch5:297 set it up, and "for several minutes, nothing happened" (6:59) is the first true quiet beat.
