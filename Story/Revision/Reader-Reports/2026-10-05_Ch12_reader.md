## What worked (keep these)

- "I know what he looks like when he is not here and somebody else is injured." (L47) Mama Baga learns the whole museum story from an arm and an absence, and the page says how she knows. It is also the first time the book lets Pathwell's mother-ness show without naming it.
- The reduction scene (L110-148): "Does being ready change anything?" / "No." / "Then why ask?" / "Because it's your shoulder." Then "All right," ... the healer waited ... "Do it." The healer treats "all right" as not-yet-consent, in the chapter right after a man who said it and meant nothing. It carries the theme in two lines and nobody explains it.
- "That wasn't the same as saying it was nothing." (L294) The one sentence in the diary reflection that is Elizabeth thinking like a person.
- The restriction and the card: "Don't let Pathwell decide the restriction expired." / "DONOR PERMISSION REQUIRED. PATHWELL IS NOT DONOR." / "He respects paperwork more consistently than people." (L413-429) Funny and cutting and it spends the Ch 1 diary-reading without a flashback.
- The meal and the nothing-moment (L211-223, L262-280): stew, a bark joke, Shade with a bandaged palm and a spoon. This is the Sunday Morning soft landing, and it comes from the people the story follows.
- The seam: the empty cot and the healer (L60-64), the turnip boy's sock three times (L13, L244, L439), "a different spoon" (L452). Small, specific and not explained.

### The seven snags, in order of a reader's notice

**S1. L325, "ONE PAGE USED".** Ch 4 L426-434 has the archivist count two missing places in that book. The card undercounts. P23 knew and kept it; the card is also a joke about records, so an official wrong number is arguably fine. Fix: add "AT CAMP", or "ONE PAGE USED SINCE ARRIVAL" is clumsy, so "ONE PAGE USED AT CAMP" is the smallest.

**S2. L203-207 "reckless" / "I have standards" / "Stansbury has infected you."** Three problems in four lines: "reckless" has no referent (she is sitting still), the "standards" and "infected" lines refer to a Ch 10 exchange she and Shade were not in (knowledge: how does she, or he, know it?), and the joke shape ("I wasn't"/"You were going to") is the same one as L164-168 and L270-276. Fix: cut the four lines; the chapter loses nothing (Craft lesson 4).
