# Reader report: Chapter 8 (read cold after Ch 1-7; hand-off Ch 9 L1-140)

**Headline.** No stoppers. Four snags, all about picturing the scene (the ditch that turns out to be concrete, light on a man fifty yards away, nobody offering to ride along, and a few dropped body details). - "Her third arrived in her body before either of the first two had finished. / Wrong." (L157-159), then "Absolutely not." (L173) and "That is a terrible start." (L191).

- "It was the crash that woke her." (L69) is placed bare, as R11 asked, right after the hands on the wheel, with nothing pointing at it.

4. L357: "somebody had finally said" becomes "somebody had finally offered pieces" (or cut "finally").
