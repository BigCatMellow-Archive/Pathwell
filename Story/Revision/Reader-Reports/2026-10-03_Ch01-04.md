## 1. What worked (keep)

- **The whole-book exchange (L258-288).** "No." / "Sorry. That was for me." / "If I keep holding the rest, I'm going to spend the next ten minutes finding a reason this page is the wrong one." Her reason is a person's reason, and it pays Chapter 3's "These were never his to spend" by having her spend it herself.
- **The held-out hand (L324-336).** "The expectation arrived automatically, the way you hold out your hand for change after paying." The consequence lands through a gesture, not a statement.
- **Pathwell and Elizabeth at the wheel (L343-361).** "She'll be fine." / "I know." / "That was-" / "I know." / "This wheel is new." / "Newer. Relatively." Both sides avoid the subject and the page lets them. This is the best use of Pathwell's humour-as-protection in the chapter.
- **Mama Baga's care given as orders (L136-154).** "Sit before you fall over politely." / "That is not a posture." The onions, then "Elizabeth nearly cried again and resented the blanket for trying." Matches the bible and Q126 (motherhood through behaviour).
- **The archive scene's purpose (L440-494).** The blue road book (HESS line) shows what an archive is for before the reader is told; "Specificity is expensive" and "That is generally the opposite of an archive" turn the sadness without cancelling it. "His hands were clean. That mattered to Elizabeth more than it should have."
- **Clean healing.** "No flash. No burst of gold." against Chapter 1's gold, and "Not burned. Spent." The page keeps the Q105/R0 6b lock (no residue) and the Chapter 1 contrast does work.
