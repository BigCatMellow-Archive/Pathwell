Pathwell tied Shade to him with a brass frame at Camp. Mama Baga said stop; he drove the pin in anyway. The frame broke, a lantern fell, and the archive wagon burned. Elizabeth, one arm in a sling, pulled Milo out from under a shelf and left her diary in the doorway. Nana's cookbook burned. Stansbury saved a notebook and some letters and burned his hand. Then the dark blob came out of the trees, smelling of low tide, for the fresh spill. Shade walked away from Pathwell to draw it off him. It took him from the legs up. The spill is gone, the blob left, and Shade didn't come back. I don't know if he's dead. I don't know what the Space Between is, or what the mirror does. Celia and the pears are gone. I think the archivist and Stansbury are different people.
