Elizabeth had a party, went to bed, and was woken by a crash. A man from the party is going through her boxes and reading her diary. Something wet and smelling of low tide breaks through the door. He wants Nana's cookbook and asks her favorite recipe: sugar cookies. His hand on the page makes it glow gold, and the light rings like a bell when it hits the blob. She hides in the bathroom while they fight. Afterward the living room is covered in ooze, and the cake is fine. He never says his name. He says her house is "marked, magically speaking," and they have to go. She asks why she heard her Nana's voice. "Because it was hers." She says she's not going anywhere. Then she puts on shoes and goes with him. "Ready?" "No." "Perfect." I don't know what the blob is.
