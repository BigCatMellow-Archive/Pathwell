Camp burns. Pathwell tied Shade to him with a brass frame and drove the pin in after Mama Baga said stop; the frame broke and the archive wagon went up. Elizabeth hurt her shoulder pulling Milo out from under a shelf. Her diary and Nana's cookbook burned. Stansbury, Pathwell's brother, burned his hand saving a notebook. Shade walked off to draw the blob away and was eaten. He's dead. Milo says he still owed him a hand at cards.

Pathwell apologized. Elizabeth won't forgive him yet and won't make his growth her job. Mama Baga set his terms: work, restitution where possible, no touching charged originals, no pruning to pay, ask before reorganizing. Stansbury turned down Pathwell's offer to reduce his scar. Elizabeth cried at night for Nana, the diary, and Shade.

The archivist (no name I caught) wrote a list with UNKNOWN on it. I don't know what the Space Between is, or what the mirror does. Celia and the pears are still gone.
