Camp burned. Pathwell tied Shade to a brass frame; the frame broke, the wagon went up, and Shade walked off to draw the blob away and was eaten. Elizabeth hurt her shoulder and lost her diary and Nana's cookbook. Pathwell is working off Mama Baga's terms, building racks for the archivist and copying the blue road notebook. Elizabeth gave a museum statement; the museum reopened, "not restored." The yellow replacement wagon is now the archive, called The Lemon. Elizabeth isn't ready to forgive Pathwell. Then she asked him to take her to the Space Between. They went in through a laundromat back door with an OUT OF ORDER sign and a brass tag. The shopkeeper has an orange cat that sits on his ledger. Elizabeth found a green cloth family cookbook, MERRITT FAMILY KITCHEN, 1948, full of feuding margin notes. Pathwell put it back and said it was his fault. She asked him to come, and he said yes. They left through a narrow brass door; something honked on the other side. Still unknown: the mirror, Celia, the pears.
