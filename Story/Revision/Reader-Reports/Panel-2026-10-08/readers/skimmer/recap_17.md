Camp burned. Pathwell tied Shade to a brass frame, the frame broke and the wagon went up. Shade walked off to draw the blob away and was eaten. He's dead. Elizabeth hurt her shoulder, and lost her diary and Nana's cookbook. Stansbury burned a hand. Pathwell apologized; Elizabeth isn't ready to forgive him. Mama Baga set his terms: work, restitution, no touching originals. Now he builds a rack for the nameless archivist, who keeps making him move things (three inches lower, brace outside). He copies the blue road notebook, smudges included. Elizabeth gives a museum witness statement. Pathwell offered the Vale letters as a replacement and was refused. The museum reopened, "not restored." The yellow replacement wagon is now the archive, called The Lemon. Pathwell lost at Hearts by twenty-six. Elizabeth is back at work, taking long walks, and wants to go somewhere. Still unknown: the Space Between, the mirror, Celia, the pears.
