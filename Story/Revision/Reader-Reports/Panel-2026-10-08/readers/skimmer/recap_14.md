Pathwell turns up at Camp hurt, gauze on his temple, ribs bad. Elizabeth's shoulder is back in, and she's still furious at him. He says her name properly this time. Shade, in his green jacket, keeps reaching his right hand toward Pathwell, like something is pulling it. Pathwell builds a brass frame with three pins and a cord to tie Shade to him. Shade says no. Mama Baga keeps saying stop. Pathwell drives the third pin in anyway. The frame breaks, a lantern falls, and the archive wagon catches fire. Elizabeth's diary is on the floor. Nana's cookbook is still on its shelf. Milo is stuck inside the burning wagon, shouting. I don't know what the Space Between is, or what the mirror does. Celia and the pears are gone.
