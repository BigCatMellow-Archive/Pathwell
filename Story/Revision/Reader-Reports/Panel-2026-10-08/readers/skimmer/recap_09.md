Elizabeth is done being dragged around by Pathwell, who keeps saying "the prune that went wrong" and won't explain. A black blob swallowed him to the wrist; she cut him out with a foam dagger. His brother Stansbury sells magic made from kids' schoolwork. Elizabeth crashed a maroon Cadillac on purpose. Then Shade, a man who looks like Pathwell, turned up with pieces of Pathwell's past and a hand that keeps turning toward him. In a 24/7 diner Shade has fragments: a Prague room he never entered, a woman named Celia who hated pears, a loose fourth stair. He doesn't want to be Pathwell. He says Pathwell chose something and won't say what. Elizabeth walks back toward the road to go find out, and not for Shade. I still don't know what the Space Between is, what the cookbook was for, or what the mirror does.
