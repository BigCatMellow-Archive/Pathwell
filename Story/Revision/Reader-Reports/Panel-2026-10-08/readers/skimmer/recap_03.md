Lizzy had a party, then woke to a man from it going through her boxes for Nana's cookbook and a sugar cookie recipe. He's Pathwell, and he buys dumplings. A wet thing that smelled like low tide came in. His hand made a page glow. Her house is marked. She went with him anyway. Then she heard Nana say "Lizzy."

Pathwell took her through a folded-paper door into a bookstore called the Space Between, full of other people's lives. She had a panic attack in an aisle, woke to an orange cat named Boots, who cut her hand. Pathwell healed it with a paper strip. A round man at the counter wants payment. Pathwell sold his potential, roots in his arm. Bill paid. Lizzy got Nana's cookbook back, with a page torn out.
