Elizabeth (Lizzy) had a party, then woke to a crash. A man from the party was going through her boxes, wanting her Nana's cookbook and her favorite recipe, sugar cookies. A wet thing that smelled like low tide came through the door. His hand on the page made it glow. She hid in the bathroom. Her house is "marked, magically speaking," and she left with him anyway. He's called Pathwell, first name only, and he buys dumplings. Her street changed: a laundromat, a park that shouldn't be there. Her dry-cleaning ticket went into the gutter and she cried. Pathwell says don't go back tonight; she says she wants to go home. Then she hears Nana say "Lizzy," smells butter and sugar, and jumps up. I think "He still has it" means the cookbook. I don't know whose "he" that is. I don't know what the blob is.
