# Top ten things I'd tell the author

1. Chapter 3: Define the currency (Rai Stones, Lydian coins) or cut it. This is where I would have quit.
2. Chapters 1 to 3: Give Nana's death a real scene early, not a passing mention in Chapter 12's diary list.
3. Chapter 3: Cut the blood-god cat joke or explain what Boots guards, and translate or drop "Perdat quod esse poterat."
4. Chapter 4: Cut the list of Camp's occupants ("the gwyllion," "the mambos," "runemasters"); it reads as a glossary.
5. Chapter 5: Fix or drop the second-person narrator slip ("You would be forgiven for not stopping in the town...").
6. Chapters 7 to 8: Answer "Which prune?" when Elizabeth asks it, or give a partial answer, instead of holding it until Chapter 10.
7. Chapter 8: Decide and state what Shade is ("Existing. Pick a word." and "Pieces of what?" "Him.") so the reader can track him.
8. Chapter 8: Cut "It was the crash that woke her," which repeats Chapter 1's first line, unless the echo is clearly deliberate.
9. Chapter 14: Thin the climax jargon ("released possibility," "ownerless threads," "fresh spillage") so the physical action stays visible.
10. Chapters 11, 13 and 17: Trim the banter before set pieces and the repeated Hearts beats (Chapter 13's round-by-round, Chapter 17's epilogue).
