# Continuity read, Chapters 1-18

Files opened: the brief, every file in the text folder (Chapter_01 to Chapter_18), and the out folder's log.md. Nothing else.

log.md already held rows for Chapters 1-9 when I started. I re-read those chapters in full, found the rows accurate, and appended my additions and the later rows without editing earlier lines.

Notation: Elizabeth = E, Pathwell = P, Stansbury = S. "R hand" means right hand.

Day count, as far as the text fixes it: Ch 2 she says "It's Thursday." Ch 5 the child answers "Friday." Ch 5 P had said "It's Saturday," which was wrong. So the nine o'clock meeting was Friday morning, which she misses sitting in a field. The Camp, bar, crash, diner, and museum events run Friday afternoon into Saturday dawn. The fire is Saturday. Ch 17-18 run on from there (day 3, 12, 14, 18, 21; Ch 18 is "Three weeks and four days after the archive burned").

## (b) Unexplained changes

1. **Pages used, cookbook.**
   Before, Ch 4: the archivist asks "Pages used?" E: "One tonight." "Before that?" "One. Also tonight." He "checked the two missing places."
   After, Ch 12: the card under the cookbook reads "ONE PAGE USED — CHICKEN & DUMPLINGS." Ch 15: "One page used."
   The count goes from two to one with no explanation. The sugar-cookie page (Ch 1) was the other.

2. **The cat scar.**
   Before, Ch 3: the claw "dragged through the soft part between her thumb and wrist"; P "rubbed his thumb over the thin line it had left behind. Unfortunately, scars are outside my wheelhouse."
   After, Ch 18: "Elizabeth looked at the base of her thumb. The old scratch was long gone."
   The scar P said he couldn't fix disappears. The spot also shifts from the back of the hand to the base of the thumb. Ch 5 does not mention it either ("rubbed the back of her hand" after S's smack).

3. **The page left behind.**
   Before, Ch 1: "Elizabeth snatched the book, leaving the page behind." The page is last seen under P's palm, with "loose threads of gold" falling.
   After, Ch 1: "A ragged edge stuck out where the page had been." Nothing says what became of the page. I don't know.

4. **Cookbook weight.**
   Ch 3: "The cookbook felt heavier, which was stupid. Missing a page should have made it lighter." The text flags this and never returns to it. I didn't care much, but a reader who flags it should be paid.

5. **Pajamas.**
   Before, Ch 1: "she'd gone straight to bed" and "jumped into her shoes, and she threw on a coat."
   After, Ch 4: "the pajama legs showing beneath it, which Elizabeth had been trying not to think about since the lobby of her building."
   Never mentioned in Ch 2, where she complains at length about what she'll wear. Pajamas "balled in one hand" become "folded on the onion crate" in the same scene.

6. **Archivist's tools.**
   Before, Ch 4: he "dipped his pen." Ch 12: "three pencils behind one ear."
   After, Ch 13: "one pencil still behind his ear." Nobody removed two.

7. **Time at Camp versus outside.**
   Ch 11, end of chapter: "Outside the high windows, it was daylight." Ch 11, seen through the threshold: "firelight, woodsmoke."
   Ch 12: "lantern light; here it was still dark." Dawn arrives only when she leaves the archive.
   The same hour is dawn or daylight at the museum and dark at Camp. Ch 5 had Camp "night" and the field outside it gray dawn. Never explained.

8. **Direction of travel on the county road.**
   Before, Ch 10: "Elizabeth walked on the shoulder facing traffic."
   After, Ch 10: "The vehicle came up behind her on the far side of the road." A van that comes up behind a walker facing traffic is not coming at her.

9. **Which side the ditch is on.**
   Before, Ch 8: "the right front wheel dropped into the ditch beside the culvert." P sits behind E (the driver); E "got out on the driver's side."
   After, Ch 8: "Pathwell shoved at the rear door. It stuck halfway against the slope of the ditch." The rear driver's-side door would face the road, not the right-hand ditch.

10. **"Twenty-five minutes."**
    Before, Ch 9, early in the meal: Shade: "I met you on the road twenty-five minutes ago."
    After, Ch 9, after the whole meal and conversation: Shade, about S: "He met me on the road twenty-five minutes ago." E: "Don't start doing that." Both are the same figure after a drive, an order, a meal, and a long talk.

11. **Ch 8 repeats Ch 1's opening line.**
    Before, Ch 1: "It was the crash that woke her."
    After, Ch 8: "It was the crash that woke her." Mid-crash, while she is driving and awake.

12. **Why the walk is "back" to the bar.**
    Ch 9: "She did know where the night had started." Ch 10: "She walked toward the bar because it was where the night had started."
    The night started at her apartment, not the bar. I read it as a slip, not a plan.

13. **Stansbury's name.**
    Ch 4-9, 11-18: "Stansbury." Ch 10 only: P: "She is our responsibility, Leo." and later "Leo, the next time I call you an idiot ... " and "Leo, you mustn't be so desperate." Nothing explains "Leo."

14. **The sling arm and the shelf.**
    Ch 12: healer: "You are not using this arm today." Ch 15: E tears the sling off and uses it. This is explained in the scene (she chooses it) and I accept it.

15. **Milo's footwear.**
    Ch 4, 12, 13: "One sock" always falling. Ch 15: "His sock had finally stayed up," his "left boot scraped the floor," then "bare right foot, one sock still somehow present on the left." Ch 16: "His left sock had fallen down again." Boots first appear in Ch 15. Not an error so much as a late arrival.

16. **Pathwell's ribs.**
    Ch 7: swallowed by the blob, no rib injury named. Ch 10: "Pathwell caught it against his chest and winced." Ch 11: "one arm across his ribs." The first named rib injury has no cause.

17. **Concussion.**
    Ch 17: S: "I am deciding whether the concussion was permanent." No earlier chapter says P had a concussion, only a temple cut (Ch 11).

18. **Coins.**
    Ch 2: "She dumped the contents of her pockets on the table." Nothing shows her picking up the coins. Ch 9: "I don't have any money." Consistent, but only because she never picks them up.

19. **Skies.**
    Ch 5: "overcast sky," "every drop of the morning's rain," then, hours later, "The low sun scattered itself through the single tree." Ch 6: "open horizon" with a clear red sky. Plausible clearing, never stated.

20. **Bandana.**
    Ch 5: "Her hand was snatched back just as her fingers closed on it," then "the bandana in her hand had been singed in half." She did not close her fingers on it. Where the other half is: not said.

## Reader's note

What I'd cut first, because it cost me trust as a puzzle-reader: the two-page versus one-page tally on the cookbook card (Ch 12), the vanished scar (Ch 18), the van that overtakes a walker who faces traffic (Ch 10), and the reuse of the first line in the crash (Ch 8).
