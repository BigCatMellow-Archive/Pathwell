# Ten things I'd most want to tell the author (1 = most important)

1. Explain, and plant early, why P was at E's party and in her boxes at the start; the whole plot hangs on it and it is never answered (Ch 1, 2, 5, 9).
2. Let the reader detect the Shade/prune answer from clues before the van confession; as written the mystery is solved by asking (Ch 6, 7, 8, 9, 10).
3. Fix the cookbook page count: two pages gone in Ch 1 and Ch 4, but "ONE PAGE USED" on the card in Ch 12 and Ch 15.
4. Fix the cat scar: P says he can't fix it in Ch 3; Ch 18 says it is "long gone," at a different place on the hand (Ch 3, 18).
5. Fix the car geometry: right-hand ditch with a rear door "against the slope" (Ch 8), and the van that overtakes a walker facing traffic (Ch 10).
6. Pay off or cut the dangling clues: the shadow in the window (Ch 2), the lavender page (Ch 3), P's whisper (Ch 15), the third scrap (Ch 10), the missed meeting (Ch 5, 17).
7. Say what happens to the dagger, the Cadillac, Shade's sedan and the box of foam weapons; I can't tell if they are gone or forgotten (Ch 7, 8, 11, 15, 16-18).
8. Trim Ch 17's restitution scenes (rack, wagon colour, crooked label); Ch 16 already made the point, and Ch 17 repeats it as a list.
9. Resolve small slips that make a rereader stop: "Leo" (Ch 10), the doubled "twenty-five minutes" (Ch 9), the repeated opening line mid-crash (Ch 8), Camp dark while the museum is daylight (Ch 11, 12), pencils changing number (Ch 12, 13).
10. State why P closes the Merritt cookbook and why the shopkeeper needs to sit down; I inferred it but the text never says, and it is the book's last real beat for him (Ch 18).
