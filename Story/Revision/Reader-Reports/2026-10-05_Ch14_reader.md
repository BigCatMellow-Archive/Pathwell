## Headline

Ch 14 is a chapter that works. A reader laughs for the first 110 lines, cares for the next 300, and braces for the last 80, and the cause of the failure is shown in the action (payment released with nowhere coherent to go; the contradiction is Pathwell's) and not only told. **Stoppers: 0. Snags: 12. Quibbles: 24. Pass 3: 2 holds, 6 breaks (all mild; two of the six are record-keeping only).** The two things that matter most are not line fixes: (a) **nothing soft lands inside Ch 14 after the fall** (L615-621 are four flat lines, then Ch 15's fire opens at full pitch); the light-touch candidate is one bucket-line beat after L613, flagged for the author; (b) **three questions of lock reading** (C1 section 6, Q145's length, the recoil choreography) that only the author can settle. Eight of the twelve snags are one-word or one-clause fixes.

## What worked

- **The argument is paid for by the characters, not the narrator.** "This is leaving damage behind you." / "Not if it works." / "You still think the result gets to decide whether the choice was acceptable." / "For who?" / "Pathwell had no answer fast enough." (L431-451). Elizabeth challenges and does not steer, which is what she is for.
- **"Do I remain myself?" / "I think the distinction can survive." / "Can." / "Not will." / "No." / "Then no."** (L329-353). The book's centre, and it is carried by one-word replies that here bear the weight of a refusal, not a tic.
- **The jokes die at the right moment.** "You are not making that joke today." / "No." / "Sorry." (L255-263) is the chapter's turn from light to heavy, and the person who turns it is the one it costs.
- **"That's what you said at the museum."** (L561-565). Ch 11's "I can contain it" returning as the line that tells the reader why he does not stop. The three "Yes." answers (L521-533) set it up.
- **The failure is a mechanism.** Payment released, structure with "nowhere coherent to put it", the cord splitting into two shadows, "One said together. One said separate." (L537-541). A reader can say why it failed.
- **The fire's catalogue** (L595-607): "Someone writing a daughter's name three times because once had not felt sufficient." and "The ordinary fire had found history, and history answered badly." This is the book's promise kept at its hardest chapter.
- **The dagger stays put for a stated reason** (L471): "whose it was to cut." It pays Ch 5 and Ch 7 by choosing not to, and it is R37 on the page. The "Prague" beat (L165-187) pays Ch 9; "Lizzy" / "Elizabeth." (L65-77) pays Ch 7 and Ch 10; Mama Baga's silent bracelets pay Ch 13.
