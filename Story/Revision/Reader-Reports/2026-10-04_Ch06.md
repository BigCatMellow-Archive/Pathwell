# Reader report: Chapter 6 (independent read, cold then informed)

**Verdict up front.** No stopper. Two small holes (both snags), one seam gap with Ch7, and a handful of quibbles and taste points. The chapter holds its picture. The hand-off to Ch7 works.

## Holes and gaps (smallest fix only)

1. **HOLE, snag. L231.** Elizabeth: "You said you came here because something didn't fit." Pathwell never said that on the page. Stansbury did (Ch5 L206, "The part that doesn't fit is why it was already at your door"), and Pathwell answers "Yes" to a line he didn't speak. Smallest fix: "Stansbury said something didn't fit."
2. **GAP (seam), snag. Ch6 to Ch7 L321.** Stansbury says "The mirror" before anyone has mentioned one. In Ch6 the mirror exists only inside Pathwell's POV (L186-216); Pathwell tells Stansbury nothing but "Later." and "Not here." It's fillable (Stansbury's regular bar, Pathwell vanished toward the back hall), but the page doesn't say he knows the hall. Smallest fix, if wanted: at L227-229, "Stansbury looked toward the back hall, then back at him."
3. **QUIBBLE. Ch7 L331-333.** "Not here." / "You said that inside." In Ch6 Pathwell said "Not here" (L255), not Stansbury. It reads if Elizabeth turns to Pathwell on the second line; ambiguous as spoken.

## What worked (keep)

- The bar's arrival: "a mounted fish ... with the exhausted expression of something that had heard all of this before. The bartender had roughly the same face."
- Elizabeth's first refusal, "That hasn't stopped me from forming an opinion." It pays R15's "the choice is yours" in miniature.
- "They knew how to occupy the same room without paying attention to each other, which was apparently different from ignoring each other."
- "He disappears constantly. Quiet is newer." Best line in the chapter.
- The dart plant: bullseye with a shaking hand, then the miss in the paneling, "The wall had been arrogant all evening." It pays P7, and it's the register guide's own dart image.
- "It sounded strange coming from Pathwell, and that was what made her believe it." The Ch5 "I don't know" gag cashes in.
- The mint-muddler thud a hair ahead of the hand (L115) is a clean plant for the last line; "Wet. Heavy." echoes Ch1.
