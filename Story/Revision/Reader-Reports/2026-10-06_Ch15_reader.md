## Pass 1b: the questions I was asked, answered from the cold read

*These are my impressions as logged cold, written down from the cold log. Where Pass 2 changed or confirmed something, I say so.*

**Why does Shade walk into the blob's path when he does, in my own words.** The blob has just reached the edge of Camp and is "assessing": it pulls toward Pathwell, toward Shade, toward the fresh spill ("three related signals occupying too little ground", L351). Around it, people are being moved back (L423): Milo drawn back by the archivist with one bare foot dragging, Stansbury getting up off the bucket with his burned hand wrapped, and Shade looks at Elizabeth's left arm "hanging out of what was left of the sling" (L425). Then the line that tells me why: "I told him to leave it unresolved. I thought I'd be the one carrying it." (L427). So: he asked for the thing to stay unresolved because he believed the only person it cost was him; it has been costing a child's leg, a burned hand, a dislocated shoulder, an archive. Walking away from everyone so the blob has to choose is how he stops it, framed as an experiment ("which problem it thinks is biggest", "By separating the signals", L429-433) because the outcome is not known. It is a decision to stop letting other people pay, taken with open eyes.

- **Sacrifice?** Not for Pathwell: he refuses Pathwell's "Stay where you are" and "Will you stay?", his look at Pathwell is neither a request nor forgiveness (L510), and he never says anything to him. It is a sacrifice only in the sense that it may cost him everything; the page never frames it as noble.
- **Miscalculation?** No. "That seems likely" (that it will hurt him) and "It isn't [a plan]" tell me he knows the odds are bad. I did not read it as a man who expected to come out fine.
- **Guilt?** No, and I looked for it. There is no line where he blames himself, and he jokes and smiles at the end (L489-499). The distinction C30 asks for ("my existence is a mistake" versus "I will not keep making other people pay") is carried by behaviour: the cards, the smile, "I was winning." One thing could tip it: "That is new. I'm not." (L373) can be read as "I'm the old waste." I had to stop on it (see snag 6).
- **Compelled by the draw?** No. The draw drags the other way the whole walk ("It made his gait ugly", L461), and "His right hand closed." (L514) is the one moment the draw is overruled.
- **Refusing to let others keep paying?** Yes. That is what I understood, and I understood it at L427, not before. Lines 353-373 are oblique: "What happens if I move away from you?" / "Good." / "Accurate." / "That is new. I'm not." I knew he was up to something and not what.
- **Does what Elizabeth carried out of the fire play a part?** Yes, as an image, and I think as a rhyme more than as a cause. She tore the sling off to lift the shelf (L116) and paid in her shoulder to carry Milo out (L148); Shade looks at exactly that arm, and Milo's foot, and Stansbury's hand, right before he says why. It made his reason concrete for me: "carrying" meant that. I would not say her choice changed his: the fire, the archive, Milo hurt and Stansbury burned were all there whether or not she went in, so remove her and he still sees all of it (the tenth seat's argument, which my cold read agrees with before having read it). Her rescue is the first of two people doing the thing (she pays herself rather than ask anyone else to; he does the same), and the book is better for the symmetry. It does not make the climax hers.

**Does the death read as the blob "correcting an anomaly"?** Mostly no, with one tilt. It reads as his choice because the blob is hesitating until he moves (it "changed course" after him, L471), the walk is the only act in the sequence, and nothing in the narration calls him waste: the spill is "removed as unclaimed waste" (L528) and he is "taken". The tilt is L536: the blob "found nothing left that it had come for". A reader who has been told the blob comes for waste hears Shade filed with the waste. It is the locked mechanism (C28: Shade is the primary cleanup target and the blob then clears the spill), so the choice here is to keep it and make sure nothing else leans that way. See quibble 11.

**A speech, a narrator explaining meaning, or a "Not X. / Y." cadence at the climax?**
- **Speech:** none. Shade's longest speech is L365 (one sentence of inference) and his reason is two.
- **Banter:** the Hearts exchange (L489-499) and the Pathwell/Shade volley (L337-349, L375-385) are character, not cadence, and I would keep every line.
- **Narrator explaining meaning (each one stopped me, small):** L98 ("Shade's voice and Pathwell's voice and her own"), L395 ("A person could accumulate a surprising amount of life in a morning."), L451 ("It wasn't forgiveness, or quite surprise. It was the recognition of an effort too small for the moment, and still real."), L483 ("Shade had never been particularly interested in deserving scenes."), L510, L514, L522 ("The silence inside him was visible."), L563.
- **"Not X. / Y." at the climax, in narration:** L451, L504 ("It didn't bite. There was no mouth."), L510 ("He didn't ask for anything, and he didn't forgive him. He just looked."), **L514 ("The draw hadn't done that. Shade had.")**, L528 ("It wasn't settled, or returned to Pathwell. It was removed as unclaimed waste."), L553-559, L563. The most exposed is L514: it states in the Not-X/Y shape what the page has just shown, at the single moment the reader is least in need of help.

**Every speaker attribution I had to stop on.**

- L367-369 "Think." / "Yes." Not a misattribution but opaque: whose "Think." (hers, picking up his "I think") and what "Yes." concedes.
- L226 "You don't need to save everything." follows a smoke sentence with no tag; I took it as Elizabeth's only when "He looked back at her." came (L228). One-second stop; the sequence reads correctly.
- Everything else (L118-132, L152-160, L206-216, L258-268, L315-323, L337-349, L353-361, L375-387, L397-419, L439-455, L489-499) attributes cleanly, including the one-word replies. No merge error found in the Elizabeth/Milo/Stansbury/Shade/Pathwell exchanges.
