# Pathwell: review against the targets

I'm an independent reviewer and I didn't write any of this text. I read the target pages, the brief, the contract matrix, the Decisions R-rows and locks, IDEA-0001 and INS-0001, then Chapters 1–18 in order. Nothing under the repository was changed. Line numbers are each chapter file's own. Anything marked *Opinion* is my judgement, not a finding against a record.

**Headline:** all ten non-negotiable scenes are on the page, and nine of them land as written. The book's main risk to its own thesis test is the back half explaining itself. The book barely names choice while the author's drafts carry it: the words chose, choice and decide appear 0–3 times per chapter in Ch1–7, but 4–10 times per chapter in Ch8–18. In Ch14 and Ch16 several characters say the argument outright, and from Ch9 on the narrator keeps labelling Elizabeth's choices as choices.

## 1. Non-Negotiable Scenes

1. **Clean prune early.** On the page in Ch3:145–275: the vow at 211–229, the debt cleared at 239 and 273, and "Did Camp Cunnan ask you for all of this?" at 157. It lands.
2. **Elizabeth cuts Pathwell out of the blob.** On the page in Ch7:49–79. The mirror turns the problem toward him at Ch6:186–245, and she takes the dagger by her own choice at Ch5:313. It lands.
3. **The cookbook given to heal a stranger.** On the page in Ch4:286–356, with the page spent at 322–324. The book's "lived life there" is only a support and a card (Ch12:303–317). It partly lands; see finding 3.
4. **The midpoint crash, chosen.** On the page in Ch8:3–69, with the echo bare at 69. It lands. *Opinion:* the consequences are thin (bruised ribs and a dead car); see finding 4.
5. **The diner.** On the page in Ch9: "never the point" at 224 and "He knows what he did. Ask him." at 336. The recognition beat moved to the roadside under W16 (Ch8:157–233). At the diner only "We used to— / He used to" (Ch9:130–136) is left of it. It lands on W16's terms.
6. **The Ask and the museum.** On the page in Ch10:392–578 ("But you still chose it." at 560, "Yes." at 564) and Ch11:299 ("I don't need to be rescued."). He overrides her at 325–363, the records are lost at 373–389, and she's hurt at 401. It lands.
7. **The forced reintegration.** On the page in Ch14. He builds the frame while talking (143–261). Shade says no (307, 311, 357), Mama Baga says stop (381), he forces it (529), and the recoil sends the lantern into the archive and starts the fire (559–571). It lands.
8. **The archive burning.** On the page in Ch15: the capture at 13, Milo at 31, the eye contact at 33–35, the dagger left unused at 37, the diary passed at 83 and the cookbook at 99, and ordinary fire at 358–372. It lands.
9. **Shade's death.** On the page in Ch15: the struggle at 13–25, the Camp man at 23, Pathwell watching at 21, Shade taken at 244–252, and the draw quiet at 405. Camp names him in Ch16:366–376. It lands. Pathwell's silence shows only at Ch15:21 and 254, which is by design (R46).
10. **The quiet refusal, confession and invitation.** On the page in Ch18: the put-back at 289–295, the confession at 324–358, the hour of browsing at 362, the invitation at 417–444 and the last lines at 460–464. It lands.

## 3. Chapter by chapter

Each chapter's contract is its W-movement in the matrix, read with the later rulings (Revision-Status maps the movements to chapters).

- **Ch1 (W01).** The job is the opening promise, the cookbook's magic, the "marked" bluff and Elizabeth being pulled along. It meets it. Gap: the second thud down the hall (177) and the shadow in her window (Ch2:47) never pay off. That's small.
- **Ch2 (W02).** The job is to breathe, and to give her the first small choice: going after her books (R12). It meets it, and it's a *therefore*. Gap: the changed neighbourhood (Ch2:51–57) is still Q-O.
- **Ch3 (W03).** The job is the clean prune and her books coming back. It meets it. The voluntary continuation moved to Ch4:15 under R31, so the matrix's exit state is stale (section 6). The link is a *but*: the leverage ends.
- **Ch4 (W04).** The job is the costly gift, Camp lived in, and "I'm coming." It meets it strongly; 440 is clean ("Not because he had her things. He didn't."). Gap: the archivist's promise of use (518) never happens on the page.
- **Ch5 (W05).** The job is Stansbury, the dagger and the Cadillac. It meets it. The link into Ch6's bar is *and then* ("I wouldn't mind a refreshment"). That's acceptable as breathing room (Writing Principles §11), and 305 plants the pull.
- **Ch6 (W06).** The job is the trio enjoying itself, then the mirror turning toward him. It meets it, with the mirror kept vague (W15). This chapter has the book's best "having before losing".
- **Ch7 (W07).** The job is that she saves him and is shut out anyway. It meets it, and 245 ("Drive.") makes the crash causal. Gap: none.
- **Ch8 (W08).** The job is the chosen crash and Shade's arrival. It meets it on W16's terms. Gap: the chapter's closing lines (385–387) state the lesson of "agency isn't control" instead of letting the cost show it.
- **Ch9 (W09).** The job is that Elizabeth takes the scene back and leaves on foot. It meets it (346–380). Gap: Shade explains Pathwell's flaw twice (294, 322) where once would carry it.
- **Ch10 (W10 and W11).** The job is the tracking bridge and the Ask. It meets it, with the coffee as the shared anchor. Gap: 550–558 says outright the diagnosis that the reply at 560–564 already proves.
- **Ch11 (W12).** The job is the museum's records lived in, then lost, and the boundary crossed. It meets it with the socks, the eggs and the soap, before their loss. The scene after she leaves is a Stansbury-side coda (508–562), which makes three non-Elizabeth sections counting Ch6 and Ch10. That's within Q140.
- **Ch12 (W13).** The job is treatment with consent and the diary given. It meets it. Gap: the cookbook's second life is narrated (317), not shown.
- **Ch13 (W14).** The job is Shade simply being alive. It meets it, and it's the strongest chapter against its contract. The deletion test passes clearly.
- **Ch14 (W15).** The job is the refusal, the forced working and the fire. It meets it. The archivist sending Milo into the archive (283) is a fine *therefore*. Gap: the theme-dense stretch at 395–441 (section 2).
- **Ch15 (W16).** The job is the four separate choices. It meets it, and Elizabeth's reasons for leaving the dagger (37) are hers. Gap: see "Ruled, not proposed".
- **Ch16 (W17).** The job is the aftermath, restitution defined by Camp, and "Let it be". It meets it. Gap: it restates what Ch14–15 showed (635, 708, 720), and the narrator glosses the naming (372, 394).
- **Ch17 (W18).** The job is behaviour as evidence. It meets it. Its structure is *and then* by design (a montage of days). Gap: 283 explains a change that the walk at 281 already shows.
- **Ch18 (WC).** The job is restraint, the confession, the hour of browsing and the invitation. It meets it. Gap: the put-back depends on the reader knowing that "can he still prune?" is a live question, and nothing after Ch14 raises it (finding 5).

## 5. Top 10 structural findings, by reader impact

1. **Ch16 retells Ch14–15.** Fix: cut Ch16:635 ("Elizabeth thought of the side window… Both things were true."), Ch16:708, and "Person is the word I have for that." (720), which the 2026-10-08 panel's target-1 reader also asked to drop. The ledger line "LIKED POCKETS" (386) has already done that work. R59's three kept lines stay.
2. **The narrator labels Elizabeth's choices as she makes them.** Writing Principles §7 asks that the ladder stay *visible*, not that it be announced. Fix: cut the label clauses at Ch9:5 (from ", and the distinction" on), Ch10:306 (second sentence), Ch15:184, Ch17:76 (second sentence), Ch17:111–113 and Ch18:436. The action in each scene already carries the choice.
3. **The cookbook's second life is told, not shown** (Non-Negotiable 3; W04). Fix: one concrete trace of use inside an existing Ch12 or Ch13 beat. **NEW TEXT**; it touches an emotional object, so it's the author's to write.
4. **NN4's crash is "chosen, not its consequences", but its consequences are light, and Ch8:385–387 says the lesson out loud.** Fix: cut "Only then did she realize she was following another man who sounded certain." and "Too late to call it an accident." The chapter would then end on the rear window and the dark (385, first sentence).
5. **Ch18's put-back relies on a question the book stops asking after Ch14.** That question is whether he has anything left to prune with (Ch3:227, "You don't have much left to give"). *Opinion:* fold it into finding 6's fix rather than adding a line. A visible tremor after Ch14 would keep the question alive into Ch18:289.
6. **Continuity of the prune's cost to his body.** This is the reviser's ground (continuity, not taste). Fix: carry the shaking from Ch14 into one existing Ch16 beat, for example 174 ("His hands lay palm-up beside him, empty."). **NEW TEXT**, a staging line only.
7. **Ch14:395–441 has four characters state the argument in under fifty lines.** *Opinion:* keep 441 and 415, which readers single out. Consider whether 437–439 ("This is leaving damage behind you." / "Not if it works.") is needed, given that 441 answers the same move.
8. **Ch17:283 explains what 281 shows.** Fix: cut the paragraph, so the walk ("She liked the walk anyway.") stands alone.
9. **The narrator's glosses after shown beats.** These are Ch12:317, Ch16:372 (from "because someone had died…"), Ch16:394 (second sentence) and Ch16:261. Fix: cut each. The scene diagnostic's Q4 applies to all of them.
10. **The sedan, the chain and the second Ch1 thud are three small loose objects.** Fix: decide what each is meant to be. A cut is cheaper than a payoff (the Ch1:177 thud could go). A payoff for the sedan or the chain would be **NEW TEXT**.

**Ruled, not proposed:**
- *Whom Pathwell could have called.* The on-page counterfactual stays thin, because a man is already pulling Shade (Ch15:23) and Shade is already shouting (Ch15:19). The author kept "someone" (R54; P68's F1 answer of Mama Baga was declined).
- *Nobody at Camp learns of the confession* (R54, Q-P).
- *The dagger has no final beat* (R59 Q3).
- *Pathwell doesn't find out whether he can still prune* (R19, Q96). Findings 5 and 6 are about how legible that is, not about changing it.
- *Length isn't a target* (R42). Every fix above is a cut or a single line inside an existing scene.
