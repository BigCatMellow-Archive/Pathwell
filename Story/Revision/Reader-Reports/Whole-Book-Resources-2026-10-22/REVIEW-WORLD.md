# World review: what's true, named and planted (Chapters 1–18)

Independent read-only review. I read canon, world_bible, character_bible, glossary, Registry, Promise Ledger, Before-You-Touch, Decisions, then Ch1–18 in order. Line numbers are from `Story/Chapters/Chapter_NN.txt` as of 2026-10-22. I didn't change anything. Opinions are labelled; the rest is evidence.

Overall, the manuscript is tight. Who holds what (the books, the dagger, the sling, the peas, the foam-sword box, the first-aid box, the two side-compartment boxes) tracks almost perfectly across chapters. Most of what I found is staleness in the reference pages. Only a handful of problems are on the page itself.

## 1. Continuity errors on the page

**Factual / time**
- **Ch17:283** "A diner at four in the morning could count." By the book's own clock the diner is early evening. Sunset at the bar (Ch6:278 "evening had settled… streaked with wisps of orange and red"), the crash under the first stars (Ch8:119), then "twenty-five minutes" to the diner (Ch9:104). After that comes over an hour of walking (Ch10:3), and only then "The drive took most of what was left of the night" (Ch11:3). *Smallest fix:* "A diner in the middle of the night could count."
- **Ch11:152 vs Ch17:156.** The card says "COAT FOUND IN HENRY VALE'S ATTIC, 1978. FAMILY SAYS HIS FATHER WORE IT TO HIS WEDDING AFTER THE WAR", which reads as if Henry was alive in 1978. But the Registry's single Henry Vale wrote letters "1882 through 1886". Born after a post-war wedding, he'd be over 110 in 1978. *Fix:* "THE VALE ATTIC, 1978" (two words). Or decide they're two Henrys and split the Registry row.

**Physical / staging**
- **Ch13:503 vs Ch14:3.** The seam appears "somewhere beyond the wagons… between two trees". The next chapter opens "The threshold opened onto the unfinished hand of Hearts", and Milo and Stansbury immediately react to the cards at Shade's boots (Ch14:17–19). Ch15:13 then has the blob come "between two trees" while it's already at Shade's boot near the frame, and the frame is near enough to the archive door for the loop to hit its lantern (Ch14:567). The card table sits "near the edge of the fire" (Ch13:202). As written, Camp's geography doesn't fit together. *Smallest fix:* cut "beyond the wagons," in Ch13:503 so the trees can be the ones at the table's edge of Camp.
- **Ch14:289 vs Ch17:288/332.** "Milo took the deck and ran toward the archive wagon". The archive then burns, and Ch16:243 says "Intake is gone…". Yet Ch17 has "The archivist had the deck", and the queen of spades turns up in Pathwell's hand. The queen can be the one laid on the card table (Ch14:55; Ch16:368 "several wet playing cards"), but the deck went into the fire. *Fix:* nothing is needed if it's a new deck. If the author wants the rhyme, it's "a new deck" (one word, NEW TEXT-lite).
- **Ch11:313 "under his shoe"** / Ch11:315 "His boot caught". Shade wears boots everywhere else (Ch13:521, Ch15:13). *Fix:* "boot".
- **Ch12:76 "the borrowed blazer".** Under R41 the clothes are a gift ("Take what you like", Ch4:362; "she is 'one of them'"). *Fix:* cut "borrowed".

**Knowledge**
- **Ch16:362** Mama Baga: "His name was Shade?" Elizabeth said "Shade." aloud at Ch13:420 with Mama Baga "standing twenty feet away. She had seen." (Ch13:436). It reads well as a ceremonial confirmation, so I don't recommend a change. Flagged only.
- **Ch10:44** "The draw finds him." This is the first time anyone uses the term, and the brothers use it as a known word. Shade has only ever said "pull" or "Something keeps telling me which way you are" (Ch8:213). It's acceptable as practitioners' vocabulary. The speaker of 44/46 is also ambiguous, since 48 is tagged Stansbury.
- **Ch17:120** "The current curator emailed Elizabeth directly." We're never told how she got the address. This is minor, and Pathwell or Stansbury supplying it is implied.

**Wording that confuses a fact**
- **Ch16:726** "…or make the diary original again". Nobody copied the diary, so "original again" means nothing. *Fix (cut):* delete "or make the diary original again,".
- **Ch15:150** "children who had spent the morning taking instructions from people carrying turnips". Milo was the one carrying the turnips (Ch4:69), and this morning he was given a grinder, cards and errands. *Fix:* cut the sentence.

## 2. Magic and world rules

- **Scars: Ch3:105 vs Ch16:619–627.** Ch3 has "Unfortunately, scars are outside my wheelhouse." (the author's line, paid in Ch18:170). Ch16 has "There are ways to reduce what remains after the skin closes." / "Ordinary ways?" / "No." / "Would they require somebody's record?" / "Possibly. Not necessarily." / "Pruning?" / "No." That last exchange implies a third, unnamed kind of magic (neither a record nor a prune), which brushes against R23. The world bible keeps scars undefined ("belongs to another branch of magic", world_bible:323). Q117 needs a fix to exist so that Stansbury can refuse it. *Smallest fix:* cut "Not necessarily." so the answer is "Possibly." The fix then stays inside the record system, and Ch3's "my wheelhouse" can read as his own limit.
- **Ch11, museum: no cleanup creature.** A working that "lost hold of its shape" (Ch11:387) should leave unclaimed casting waste (world_bible:349), and Pathwell and Shade are in the same room. The brothers stay for hours. Nothing comes, and that's consistent with "Small spills can dissipate" (world_bible:87). It's not a break. I note it because it's the one place a rules-minded reader may ask.
- **Ch1:105–117: how the first blob died.** There are "sounds of struggles… light… a pop", and the creature is exploded. In Ch7:31 Pathwell can't fight one because he "brought zero books". In Ch1, his only charged material was the cookbook (exactly two pages are missing, Ch4:462, and neither went on this), the diary, and the "open box of old notebooks" (Ch1:9). The page never says what he spent. This is left open, and I don't recommend filling it (R23). If the author wants a cue, the notebooks are already on the page.
- **World bible says the museum injury came from mixing sources** (world_bible:188: "he mixes multiple emotional sources and hurts himself"). The chapter uses one quarantine letter (Ch11:341), and the damage comes from ordinary destruction feeding the release (Ch11:373, 387). The bible is stale. The chapter is fine and follows the grain rule well: "Care that meant stay away" becomes a physical distance.
- **World bible, "Coda contrast" (world_bible:55)**: "Pathwell later begins a prune… and deliberately stops before release." This is stale against R19 and Ch18:289–295 (no prune begun).
- **World bible Ch1 (world_bible:93)** "Pathwell has wandered into Elizabeth's apartment while scavenging". This is stale against R10/R48: he's a guest at her own welcome party (Ch1:7–9).
- **Canon beat 2** ("abruptly leaves/runs out of the bar") is stale against W15 and R22 (Ch6:218, a quiet return). **Canon beat 6** (diner recognition) is stale against W16 (Ch8:233). W16 is already listed in Decisions, but canon.md doesn't flag it.
- No breaks found in the core laws. Elizabeth never casts (Ch7 uses a pre-imbued tool, Q106; Ch15:366 "No spell"). Consumption destroys the record (Ch4:322, Ch10:195). Contact is required (Ch10:137/187). Ch14:499–505 is clean cancellation before release, and Ch14:529 is a forced release of a split intent. Ch15:256 has the blob take Shade, then the threads, then check Pathwell and withdraw. These match canon §Pruning and world_bible:95 closely.
