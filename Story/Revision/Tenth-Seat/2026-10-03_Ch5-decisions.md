# Minority report: Chapter 5 decisions

## D1: open with the school arrival

**Claim.** The record supports replacing the opening with the portal-to-mop-closet arrival, and its portal, "Friday" and dawn walk break no lock.

**Assumptions.** (1) A scene written for the fortune errand survives Q121 (Pathwell already means to see Stansbury). (2) A Pathwell-made portal doesn't undercut Ch9-10's road trips or the museum wall. (3) The clock closes. (4) A full scene beats the Plan's bridge.

**Weakest.** 2, then 3.

**Strongest alternative.** Keep the current opening and add only the Plan's bridge: one or two lines for "the missing day, and what became of the meeting" (Plan l.120).

**For the alternative.**
- R23: "existing text and existing canon are preferred over new material."
- The cold read's complaint was logistics ("I lost the clock"), not the scene.
- Audit Ch11: "Do not create a universal portal mechanic". R0§20a: Camp "must be reached through a reliable anchor", which is why the wall exists.

**Against the alternative.**
- Plan l.108 and Pass-Log P20 earmark the docx school half "for that pass"; W14 rebuilds from his drafts.
- It causes the waiting gag the current opening only presupposes.
- Ledger L1 ("wants one line") is met, and Friday fits Ch2 ("It's Thursday"; "meeting at nine tomorrow").
- The bible says improvised thresholds "drift", so "where you need to go" can be drift, not fate.

**Findings.**
- "I have no idea" came from the fortune errand. Now Mama Baga says "Go see your brother" and Stansbury is already briefed (Ch5). Pathwell can be vague about the place, not the goal.
- A bell, "twenty minutes" and "done for the day" conflict with a dawn walk unless the hours are accounted for.
- Ch9-11 need Pathwell to have no general portal.
- "Mrs. Pathwell" is an early Elizabeth rung; the matrix has W05-06 "still gets managed". Defensible (Writing Principles 7).

**Cost if wrong.** Contained to Ch5 plus the portal precedent; cheap to fix now, costly once Ch9-11 are read against it.

**Verdict: YELLOW. Do it if:**
- (a) One clock line pins arrival at the dismissal bell, same Friday, and "twenty minutes" is made to fit.
- (b) The portal is a one-off: aimed at Stansbury, landing wrong, costing a sheet, never reused. "It would have taken you home" stays out.
- (c) The scene stops at "wait at his car".
- (d) The meeting gets one line.

## D3: Stansbury's imbuing source

**Claim.** Naming children's latent work-energy as his source contradicts no lock and helps the book.

**Assumptions.** (1) Q106 (imbuing resource "undefined unless later story evidence requires") permits it. (2) "The kids get their work back" doesn't contradict the bible's "Using a charged work destroys the original". (3) A renewable supply doesn't cheapen the scarce-record economy. (4) It reads as characterization, not an unpaid hook.

**Weakest.** 2, then 4.

**Strongest alternative.** Leave the source undefined. W05 says "no need to define exact imbuing resource source"; nothing downstream uses the school (Ch7:95 only); no reader asked.

**For the alternative.**
- "Dead battery": work directed toward fuel is useless.
- The consent axis (Ch16:780) and Stansbury's Ch14:737 ("you decided somebody else's problem required your method") sit awkwardly beside a man who takes children's energy unasked.

**Against the alternative.**
- Current Ch5 asks "What does he do here?" and drops it.
- W05 wants "habits/work" for him; Writing Principles 15: no foil.
- Thesis: he "avoids responsibility and calls it prudence"; "It wouldn't ever be used otherwise" has that voice, and mirrors the prune rationale in the author's Ch5 draft ("you just redirect it").
- Children's work already carries charge (Ch4:394; Ch18:295-299).

**Cost if wrong.** Low; nothing depends on it, so it trims to a line.

**Verdict: YELLOW. Use only the author's words, and:**
- (a) Add one bible line: the energy is what a working throws off, not the finished piece's anchored charge.
- (b) Stansbury doesn't direct the work ("hasn't taught in years" stays).
- (c) It feeds tools only, never healing or Pathwell's workings.
- (d) No narrated verdict; Elizabeth's "harmless… I guess?" stays hedged.
- (e) Add a ledger row (open-deliberate); list it for the author as settling Q106.
