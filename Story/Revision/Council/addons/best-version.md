# Brief add-on: the best version

Used in council round 1 (2026-10-28). Append this text, unchanged, after the "Finish with your ranked top ten" line of any member's brief when the question is how to make the book its best, not only what's wrong. It names no goals and no rulings, so the brief stays blind.

````text
**One more section (put it after your top ten): the best version**

Don't stop at what's wrong. If this were your book to make into the best version of itself, what would you do? Give a prioritized plan:

1. **What to protect.** The things that must not be lost in revision, quoted.
2. **The changes, in order of impact.** For each: what to change, where (chapters), what it would do for a reader, and roughly how big a job it is (small, medium or large).
3. **The shape of the best version.** In a paragraph or two, describe the book as it would read after your changes, as a reader would experience it.

Stay concrete. Describe the direction of each change; don't write the new prose.
````
