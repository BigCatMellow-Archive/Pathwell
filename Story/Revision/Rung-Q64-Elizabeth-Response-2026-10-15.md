# Rung Q64: Elizabeth's response to Pathwell

Date: 2026-10-15. Status: author idea for testing, not revised canon.

Author suggests Elizabeth tries to relate after Pathwell admits he saw Shade pleading and knowingly did not summon help. Her proposed meaning: 'I did too, and then I heard those kids in the fire, and had to make a choice.' In the current chapter only Milo is trapped; retain Milo unless separately changed.

Critical distinction: both heard Shade calling, but Elizabeth chose to rescue Milo from immediate fire; Pathwell chose inaction because Shade's removal seemed convenient. Shared guilt and distress need not mean identical responsibility. Keep both truths readable without having Elizabeth excuse Pathwell or the narrator judge for her.

A test line might be: 'I heard him too,' Elizabeth said. 'And then I heard Milo. I went inside.' Follow with a line or pause that acknowledges Pathwell was not faced with the same competing rescue. Alternative: start 'I didn't help him either' to retain her initial emotional identification, but this risks implying moral equivalence.

Chapter 18's cookbook put-back stays silent in the test. The later admission conflicts with earlier no-explanation Q94-Q95 and must be authorized before manuscript revision. It also creates an obligation to reconsider the light final route choice and Elizabeth's wish to travel with him.
