## The goals (from memory)

- **Sunday Morning:** "You do not need to brace yourself." An interesting place, people you like spending time with, a simple problem. Small stakes with something big behind them.
- **Irreverence with heart,** like Scrubs: one habit that's funny in one light and hurts in another.
- **Write how people talk,** in the narration too. A dry narrator who notices the wrong thing at the right moment. Objects carry the feeling. Nobody explains the magic or the theme.
- **The controlling idea:** control is not competence. Elizabeth matters without any power. Pathwell spends his future and calls it help. Shade is a person.
