## What was pruned

- **First pass, most of Ch3 and some of Ch1–2: too much of the current text.** The first growth leaned on the existing chapters' lines (the thirteen-stairs memory, Boots's tribute, the handcart exchange, the party paragraph). That broke "fresh soil", so those passages were regrown. The remaining overlap with the current chapters is about 2% of six-word phrases, almost all of it soil lines.
- **"For once."** after "didn't say anything" at the end of Ch3. It was a narrator button on a gesture ending.
- **"It's perfect."** for the cake. "Perfect" is saved for the Ch1 and Ch18 bookend (line-notes rule 13).
- **Repeated phrasing** the freshness checker found ("he looked at the cake" three times, "four in the morning" twice, "my landlord is going to kill me" twice).

## Soil lines kept on purpose
