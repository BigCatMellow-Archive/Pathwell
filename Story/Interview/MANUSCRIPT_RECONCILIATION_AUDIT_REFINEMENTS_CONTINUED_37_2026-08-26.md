## Existing-story check: Q93 requires concrete Camp restitution, but the coda still does not tell the reader what Pathwell actually did during the three-week gap

Q93 locked that Elizabeth's final decision to initiate another journey is not based only on one stopped pruning impulse and `It was my fault.` During the three weeks after the archive catastrophe, Pathwell accepts Camp's terms and performs real restitution without pruning or another magical shortcut.

Current Chapter 12b already supplies the obligation: Stansbury tells Pathwell `You owe the camp for the archive` and `you owe her for the rest.` The archive itself is physically destroyed, with irreplaceable records lost. Current Coda simply jumps to `Outside, three weeks had passed` and does not yet provide the reader any evidence of how Pathwell spent those weeks.

The missing piece should stay small. The story does not need a separate rebuilding subplot or a forgiveness ceremony; it needs enough on-page information that Elizabeth's renewed companionship rests partly on observed behavior rather than an unexplained reset.

## Audit Question 100 — How should the coda establish that Pathwell actually spent the three-week gap making restitution at Camp?

### A. Give one concise narrated statement near the start of the coda that he spent the interval doing Camp-directed repair/restitution without shortcuts.

The narration can establish, without dramatizing a separate scene, that Pathwell stayed at Camp under its terms, helped rebuild/repair what could be rebuilt, helped replace recoverable material or practical infrastructure, and did not prune his way out of the debt. Irreplaceable records remain gone. Exact chores and prose can remain for manuscript reconciliation.

### B. Rely only on physical traces on Pathwell — work-worn hands, smoke/lumber residue, repaired clothes, or similar details.

This is subtler, but those details are too ambiguous to carry the important relational fact that he accepted Camp's terms and stayed to make restitution rather than merely being present around cleanup.

### C. Have Elizabeth or the shopkeeper state what Pathwell has been doing for three weeks.

This is clear, but turns dialogue into exposition and risks making Elizabeth verbally certify his improvement immediately before the ending.

### D. Leave the three weeks undescribed and let the reader infer restitution from Pathwell's later behavior.

This preserves maximum economy, but reopens the Q93 problem: Elizabeth's final invitation would again rely mostly on the coda's single live scene rather than established sustained behavior.

### Audit lean: **A**

A is the minimum explicit evidence that pays off the debt already planted in Chapter 12b. It does not require showing Pathwell's whole transformation off-page; it only establishes that after being told he owed something, he stayed and began paying it in ordinary, non-magical ways. The coda can then remain focused on the more important live choices: leaving the substitute cookbook unresolved, admitting `It was my fault`, and following Elizabeth into a new possibility. B is probably too obscure for a causal bridge this important; C over-explains through dialogue; D makes the renewed companionship feel insufficiently earned.
