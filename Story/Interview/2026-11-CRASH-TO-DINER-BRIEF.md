The author explicitly rejects an implausible leap to “this must be a clone of Pathwell.” The exact recognition trigger remains unset; the August diner-recognition mechanism is historical evidence, **not automatically restored**.

## Practical dependencies — do not solve silently

1. **The bluff must create the crash.** Exact dialogue/steering, speed, road geometry and level of injury are unset. If another blob emerges, the immediate collision may become a normal evasive maneuver, undermining the present **threat/loss-of-control** direction. The [blob rules](../Story_Files/world_bible.md#the-blobs) also require a plausible waste signal and arrival.
2. **A magical airbag requires material.** In [world_bible.md](../Story_Files/world_bible.md#charge-meaning-and-effect), genuine handwritten care/protection material can support protection. A practitioner must **touch and consume** the original source; spellcasting has skill, meaning and time constraints. A free reflexive force field is **not** established. If Pathwell takes one of Elizabeth's treasured physical recipe pages without asking, the loss is a major character/continuity consequence, not interchangeable fuel.
3. **Cookbook links Scene #3, #4 and #8.** The idea of Elizabeth saving one or two pages from Nana's cookbook is still exploratory. The **sugar-cookie recipe napkin** in [§15](AUTHOR_GOALS_INTERVIEW_2026-11-19-20.md#15-pause-page-echoes-sugar-cookie-napkin-and-written-emotion) is a *separate* mundane transcription using an already-existing fading magical memory echo. The page consumed in early Chapter 1 cannot be spent a second time.
4. **Physical consequences remain.** A wreck that leaves the brothers incapacitated while Elizabeth can walk requires credible severity and a coherent imperfect shield. “Concussed” is an author-proposed narrative condition, not established medical fact; if adopted, symptoms and impairment must follow her to the car/diner.
5. **Roadside reveal and diner continuity conflict.** Current [Ch8](../Chapters/Chapter_08.txt) contains a long roadside exchange, Shade naming himself, and an informed departure. [Ch9](../Chapters/Chapter_09.txt) refers to that choice. The August interview's **diner recognition** and the later **W16 roadside working decision** were already in tension; **R55** says the author was unsure and W16 stands as a *reviser's lean*. Do not amend Chapters 8–9 or later pursuit references from this brief.
6. **Shade's conduct remains ambiguous.** “More or less kidnapped” reflects the user's intended atmosphere. A disoriented passenger getting into a stranger's car isn't necessarily informed agreement, but it isn't the original scene's overt magical restraint either. Do not impute a settled intent (predatory, benevolent, etc.) to Shade.
