## Q140 — Elizabeth is the dominant POV with a very small number of deliberate Pathwell-limited exceptions — LOCKED B

- POV switches should be clearly staged at scene/section boundaries rather than sliding between minds within the same continuous passage.

## Q141 — Pathwell does not need to identify the exact historical prune that created Shade — USER CORRECTION / LOCKED
