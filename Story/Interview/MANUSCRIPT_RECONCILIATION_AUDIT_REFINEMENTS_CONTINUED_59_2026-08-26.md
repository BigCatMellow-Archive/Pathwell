## Existing-story check: Chapter 5 has Stansbury arrive with prepared imbued weapons, but no support establishes when Pathwell told him what was needed

Current `Chapter_05.txt` begins with Pathwell and Elizabeth waiting outside a school. After about twenty minutes, Stansbury appears from the loading dock carrying a cardboard box. He identifies its contents as weapons they need: foam swords and daggers that have already been magically imbued.

Q121 now establishes that Pathwell had already decided after the Chapter 1 blob incident that Stansbury should be brought in for defensive help. However, no repo search located a lock establishing when Pathwell actually contacted Stansbury, what he told him, or why Stansbury already has the appropriate defensive tools prepared when they meet.

The exact duration/cost/process of imbuing is intentionally undefined. Therefore the reconciliation should not casually imply that Stansbury can prepare a whole box of working tools during the twenty-minute wait unless the story deliberately wants to establish that capability.

The issue is logistical rather than magical: **when does Stansbury receive enough warning to arrive prepared?**

## Audit Question 122 — When should Pathwell contact Stansbury so the prepared weapon box makes sense?

### A. Pathwell contacts Stansbury soon after the Chapter 1 blob incident, before completing the Space Between/Camp obligation, and gives him a minimal warning to prepare defensive tools for a later meeting.

This follows naturally from Q121: Pathwell already knows he wants Stansbury involved, so he also gives him advance notice. Stansbury can prepare the tools on his own schedule while Pathwell handles the Camp obligation. The exact communication method can remain mundane and light — call/message/etc. — unless manuscript voice requires something else.

This avoids defining how quickly imbuing works and makes the Chapter 5 meeting clearly arranged rather than coincidental.

### B. Pathwell contacts Stansbury only after leaving Camp; Stansbury prepares the weapons before meeting them.

This keeps all recruitment action after Camp, but potentially compresses Stansbury's preparation into a short interval. That may be fine, but because imbuing duration is intentionally undefined, it risks accidentally implying rapid manufacture.

### C. Stansbury already keeps a standing box of pre-imbued foam weapons ready and needs no advance warning.

This makes the logistics easy and fits his defensive specialization, but establishes a new character/world fact: Stansbury routinely maintains an arsenal of charged toy weapons even when no incident is active.

### D. Remove the prepared-box idea; Stansbury joins first and obtains/prepares weapons later.

This avoids contact timing but weakens the Chapter 5 introduction of his defensive philosophy and delays the dagger setup needed for Elizabeth's later rescue of Pathwell.

### Original audit lean: **A**

A was initially favored as the smallest causal completion of Q121 because it avoided assuming rapid imbuing or a permanent cache.

## Q122 — Stansbury routinely keeps pre-imbued foam weapons as working inventory — LOCKED C, REFINED

User clarification: Stansbury's imbued weapons are **a source of income for him**.

- Stansbury routinely makes/keeps pre-imbued foam swords, daggers, and similar defensive tools as inventory connected to his livelihood.
- Therefore the Chapter 5 box does **not** need to have been prepared specifically for Pathwell after Chapter 1.
- Pathwell does not need to give Stansbury advance warning merely to explain why working weapons are already available.
- The ready inventory is not evidence that imbuing is instant or that a whole box can be prepared during the twenty-minute wait.
- This also gives the deliberately toy-like weapons a practical reason to exist outside this one plot incident: they are part of Stansbury's ordinary work rather than bespoke emergency props.
- Do not overdefine the business yet: exact clientele, prices, sales channel, production rate, and whether this is his sole or only major income source remain open unless the manuscript later requires them.
- Q121 still stands: Pathwell had already decided he wanted Stansbury's defensive help after the Chapter 1 blob incident, but that decision is separate from Stansbury already having suitable inventory.
