## Restore the full two-part locating mechanism — LOCKED A

- Preserve the earlier manuscript's **two-stage** locating working rather than simplifying it to grocery lists alone.
- **Stage one: connection.** A handwritten letter carrying longing/unrequited love provides the emotional charge for reaching toward a specific person. Pathwell must focus on a genuine shared memory, experience, phrase, or sensory association that Elizabeth might also be touching mentally. The connection does not automatically succeed merely because he knows her name.
- **Stage two: location.** Once the connection catches, handwritten grocery/shopping lists provide the practical locating/following function. Their mundane intent — identifying, seeking, and obtaining specific things — is shaped into a route/location signal.
- Both materials are consumptive magical resources. The longing letter is spent establishing the connection; grocery lists are spent maintaining the track.
- The tracking signal is temporary and fades. Additional grocery lists may be consumed to refresh or sustain it, preserving the old-draft limitation.
- This prevents locating magic from becoming effortless universal GPS: Pathwell needs a real interpersonal anchor, the target must intersect that anchor closely enough for the connection to catch, and the physical text supply is finite.
- The spell demonstrates the broader emotional-text system rather than creating a separate tracking subsystem.

## Existing-story search for the shared-memory anchor

The current manuscript already contains several possible shared anchors, but one is especially well supported:

- Chapter 3 has Elizabeth and Pathwell share **notably terrible coffee at the Space Between**. Elizabeth drinks it while recovering from the shop's emotional pressure; the taste is described as cheap, overused-filter coffee associated with late-night work.
- Chapter 9 independently gives Elizabeth another cup of **notably terrible diner coffee** immediately before/during the Shade conversation.
- This creates an existing sensory bridge: the diner coffee can naturally make Elizabeth think of the Space Between coffee at the same time Pathwell is cycling through shared experiences trying to establish the locating connection.
- This does not require telepathy. The longing working only succeeds when both happen to touch the same shared association; the coincidence is motivated by Elizabeth physically encountering a similar sensory stimulus.

Other genuine shared experiences exist — the dumpling stop, the crash, the opening `Ready? / No. / Perfect.` exchange — but the bad-coffee echo currently has the strongest built-in reason for Elizabeth to be thinking about it at the exact time Pathwell needs the connection.

## Audit Question 60 — Does the longing stage nudge Elizabeth toward the shared memory?

**A. No. The connection is passive.** Pathwell cycles through shared memories, but the longing letter cannot place, suggest, or heighten a thought in Elizabeth. The connection catches only when Elizabeth independently reaches the same memory. The diner coffee provides the concrete sensory reason she reaches the Space Between coffee at the right time.

**B. A very weak associative nudge.** The spell cannot transmit content, words, or images, but it can make an already-existing associated memory feel slightly more salient — the equivalent of something being on the tip of her tongue. Elizabeth still has to supply the memory herself.

**C. Restore the old draft's stronger nudge.** Pathwell can send the chosen thought/memory outward toward Elizabeth as an itch that may trigger it, though he cannot force her to accept or dwell on it. This preserves more of the old scene but introduces a limited form of mental influence.

**D. Remove simultaneity as a requirement.** A genuine shared memory is enough to identify Elizabeth; she does not need to be thinking about it at the same time. This avoids thought influence but makes the connection substantially easier and removes the bad-coffee timing mechanism.

Audit lean: **A**. The terrible-coffee echo already solves the simultaneity problem organically, so a mental nudge is unnecessary. Passive overlap keeps the spell difficult, preserves Elizabeth's interior autonomy, and makes the successful connection feel earned rather than induced.
