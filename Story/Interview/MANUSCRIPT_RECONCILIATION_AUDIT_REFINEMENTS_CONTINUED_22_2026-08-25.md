## Audit Question 83 — What emotional grain should the consumed letter carry?

### Context

Shade is not healing Elizabeth's shoulder back to normal. He is using one specific inherited procedure to set/stabilize it so the Camp healer can check it and Elizabeth remains in a sling and substantial pain afterward. The charged page should therefore support **holding, staying, maintaining, or keeping something from coming apart**, rather than broad restoration.

### Options

**A. Promise / endurance / commitment.** An authentic letter centered on staying, holding on, keeping a promise, or remaining through difficulty. Its emotional and contextual grain naturally supports a temporary set-and-hold working.

**B. Reconciliation / repair.** A letter about mending a damaged relationship. This aligns with repair, but risks making the magic read as fuller healing rather than stabilization and leans heavily on metaphor.

**C. Protection / caretaking.** A letter written from someone trying to keep another person safe. This can support stabilization, but overlaps the cookbook's established care/protection register and is less specifically about holding a structure in place.

**D. Generic romantic/familial love.** Emotionally strong, but too broad by itself. Under the locked magic rules, intensity without matching meaning/context should not automatically produce the precise orthopedic effect.

### Audit lean: **A**

A best matches the mechanical scope already locked for the scene: **hold this where it belongs; keep it from worsening; do not erase the injury.** It also gives the letter's destruction a coherent relationship between human meaning and magical use rather than treating emotional charge as interchangeable fuel. B risks implying repair beyond the intended stabilization; C is viable but duplicates an already prominent protective register; D is too nonspecific for the established system.
