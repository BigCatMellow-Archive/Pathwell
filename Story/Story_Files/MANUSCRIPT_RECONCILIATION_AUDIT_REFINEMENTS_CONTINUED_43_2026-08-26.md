## Existing-story check: Elizabeth must be able to use Stansbury's imbued dagger without herself performing magic

Locked character canon says Elizabeth does **not** perform magic: she does not prune, imbue, cast emotional-text magic, or possess a hidden magical ability. Her agency beats must remain ordinary human choices/actions.

At the same time, another locked beat requires Elizabeth to cut Pathwell out of the bar blob using **Stansbury's imbued toy dagger**. The dagger is specifically meant to be the tool Stansbury reluctantly armed her with, and its later usefulness is part of his character payoff.

Current `Chapter_05.txt` demonstrates one of Stansbury's foam swords by having Stansbury say something under his breath before swinging it and producing a magical effect. That staging can imply that the wielder must actively cast/trigger the magic. If that rule carried over to the dagger, Elizabeth could not independently use it without violating her hard non-magical boundary.

The world bible acknowledges `imbue` as a distinct magical action but does not currently define the activation rule for a prepared/imbued tool. This is therefore a real causal/mechanical gap rather than a need to fully systematize the entire discipline.

## Audit Question 106 — How should Stansbury's imbued weapons activate when Elizabeth uses the dagger?

### A. The magical work is done in advance by Stansbury; once imbued, the weapon's narrow effect triggers through ordinary physical use, so a non-practitioner can wield it without casting.

Stansbury creates/prepares the magical tool. Elizabeth later only swings/cuts with it. She does not supply charge, speak a spell, shape the effect, or perform an activation working. The object's prepared enchantment does the magical part. Current Chapter 5's muttered words can be treated as Stansbury controlling/demonstrating that particular sword or as staging to revise; they are not a universal requirement for every use of an imbued tool.

The exact resource cost, duration, and recharge mechanics of imbuing can remain iceberg lore unless later scenes require them. What must be fixed is that **using a pre-imbued tool is not itself spellcasting by the wielder**.

### B. Stansbury must remotely trigger the dagger's magic while Elizabeth physically uses it.

This keeps Elizabeth technically non-magical, but it makes her rescue dependent on Stansbury's simultaneous magical action and weakens the locked agency beat that she independently chooses to cut Pathwell free.

### C. Elizabeth personally activates the imbued dagger's magic when she swings it.

This makes the scene straightforward mechanically, but directly violates the hard rule that Elizabeth does not perform magic and turns one of her major human-agency beats into magical ability.

### D. The dagger's later rescue is entirely mundane; the imbuing does not matter when Elizabeth uses it.

This preserves Elizabeth's non-magical status, but discards the established Stansbury payoff that the reluctantly imbued toy dagger is specifically what becomes capable of saving Pathwell.

### Audit lean: **A**

A preserves both existing locks without requiring a large new magic system. Stansbury is the practitioner who makes the tool magical; Elizabeth is the person who decides to use the tool. Her agency remains physical and human. B transfers part of the rescue back to Stansbury, C breaks Elizabeth's central boundary, and D removes the weapon setup/payoff relationship.

## Q106 follow-through — pre-imbued tools can be used non-magically — LOCKED A

- Stansbury performs the magical preparation/imbuing in advance.
- Once prepared, the weapon's narrow imbued effect can trigger through ordinary physical use.
- A non-practitioner can therefore wield the finished tool without casting, shaping magic, supplying charge, or becoming a practitioner.
- Elizabeth's use of the dagger remains a physical human agency beat: **Stansbury made the tool magical; Elizabeth chose to use it.**
- Current Chapter 5's muttered words are not a universal activation requirement and may be revised as demonstration/control staging during manuscript reconciliation.
- Exact imbuing resource cost, duration, recharge, and broader discipline mechanics remain undefined unless later story evidence requires them.
