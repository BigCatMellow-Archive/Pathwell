## Final confrontation occurs directly outside the Camp archive — LOCKED

- **The final Pathwell/Shade confrontation takes place in the open Camp area immediately outside the archive wagon.**
- This location follows directly from established actions and mechanics rather than being chosen for climax convenience.
- Pathwell arrives at Camp with Stansbury and, before confronting Shade, goes into the archive to negotiate for Elizabeth's donated diary.
- The archive legitimately sells/trades the diary back to Pathwell. Pathwell pays with the already-locked clean prune of his own future possibility and exits the archive physically possessing the diary.
- That successful prune sends the established pain/jolt through Shade and simultaneously surges through the draw, making the deterministic pull toward Pathwell sharply harder to resist.
- **Shade is therefore physically pulled away from his tree-line position and toward Pathwell at the archive.** The draw supplies no plan, thoughts, distance, or telepathic information; it simply compels Shade toward Pathwell.
- Elizabeth follows/joins because of her established decision to be present when Pathwell decides. She remains witness rather than Shade's advocate.
- Causal convergence: **Pathwell enters archive → buys diary with clean prune → Shade feels jolt/draw surge → Pathwell exits carrying diary → Shade is pulled toward Pathwell → Elizabeth comes to witness → confrontation occurs outside archive.**
- This preserves existing Camp geography and places the failed absorption recoil close enough to physically ignite the archive without a contrived long-distance transfer.
- The stale Chapter 12b version in which Elizabeth pulls the diary from her own bag during the fire is invalid.
- No manuscript chapter has been edited; this is a reconciliation lock only.

## Pathwell offers the bought-back diary; Elizabeth refuses it — LOCKED

- **After exiting the archive with the bought-back diary, Pathwell offers it back to Elizabeth.**
- Elizabeth refuses to take it because she already deliberately surrendered it. Preserve the idea in concise language such as **`I gave that away.`**
- Her refusal is not a second long argument and does not require her to demand that Pathwell reverse the transaction. The simple fact of her prior choice is enough.
- Pathwell is left physically holding the object he spent some of his own future possibility to recover for her, even though she does not want that choice undone.
- This sharpens the climax's first control beat: Pathwell believes a legitimate transaction plus personal sacrifice makes the restoration right; Elizabeth's refusal demonstrates that paying the cost did not grant him authority to reverse the meaning of her choice.
- **When Pathwell turns from the diary toward the absorption/reintegration working, he needs his hands free and sets the diary on the archive step/threshold or an immediately adjacent archive surface by the open doorway.**
- The later Pathwell-centered recoil knocks/blows the diary back into or across the archive entrance as the archive physically ignites.
- The diary therefore becomes visibly and physically recoverable during the fire through ordinary staging, not through magic and not because it somehow returned to Elizabeth's bag.
- This produces the clean object chain: **donation → legal buyback → Pathwell offers it → Elizabeth refuses → Pathwell sets it down → failed absorption/recoil drives it into the burning archive area → Elizabeth later chooses the child instead.**
- No manuscript chapter has been edited; this is a reconciliation lock only.
