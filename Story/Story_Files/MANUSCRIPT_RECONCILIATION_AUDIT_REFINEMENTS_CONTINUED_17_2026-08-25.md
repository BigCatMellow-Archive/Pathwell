## Audit Question 76 — How does the donated diary become physically reachable during the archive fire?

### Context

Elizabeth gave the diary to the Camp archive and never gets it back. When Pathwell's failed forced reintegration ignites the archive, a Camp child is already farther inside on an ordinary errand. Elizabeth's climax still requires her to run past the diary while it is clearly saveable, recognize it immediately, and continue toward the child.

### Options

**A. The recoil physically disrupts the archive's front storage and spills the diary into the entrance aisle.** A shelf, box, or archival container is knocked open/over by the blast; the intact diary lands where Elizabeth must pass it. This is ordinary physical displacement caused by the already-locked recoil, not magical targeting. The diary remains saveable until ordinary fire reaches it during the rescue.

**B. The diary is already displayed/stored openly on a front shelf.** Elizabeth simply sees it in its normal archive location. This is simple, but requires the archive to keep a private donated diary unusually exposed rather than in ordinary contained storage.

**C. An archivist is carrying the diary when the blast hits and drops it near the entrance.** This solves placement but invents a coincidental reason for someone to be handling Elizabeth's specific diary at exactly that moment.

**D. Elizabeth has temporarily reclaimed the diary before the confrontation.** This would directly contradict the newly locked removal of the buyback/reclamation sequence and the uninterrupted donation arc.

### Audit lean: **A**

A uses a consequence already present in the scene—the violent recoil physically wrecks part of the nearby archive—to solve the placement problem without adding a new coincidence or reversing the donation. It also preserves the important distinction between **displacement** and **destruction**: the blast exposes the diary, but Elizabeth still has a real opportunity to save it; ordinary fire takes it only after she chooses the child. The main staging constraint is that the displacement must feel random/physical rather than as though the magic deliberately presents Elizabeth with a thematic test.
