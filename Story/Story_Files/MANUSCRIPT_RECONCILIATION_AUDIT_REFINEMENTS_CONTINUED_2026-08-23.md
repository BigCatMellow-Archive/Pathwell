## Shade predicts Pathwell's pattern; the draw remains central — LOCKED

- **Shade does not know Pathwell's exact absorption/reintegration plan before Pathwell states or reveals it.**
- Shade has no ongoing Pathwell memory feed, telepathy, or access to Pathwell's current planning.
- Shade can nevertheless make a strong prediction from what he legitimately has: creation-time Pathwell fragments, Pathwell's established habits, and everything Shade has personally witnessed.
- The prediction is broad and character-based: **Pathwell will come, and Pathwell will try to fix him/control the problem.** Shade does not yet know the exact method.
- **The draw remains one of Shade's defining conditions and must stay active in this sequence.** It is the deterministic compulsion orienting Shade toward Pathwell, not an information channel that tells Shade what Pathwell is thinking.
- As Pathwell approaches/converges on Camp, the draw can physically orient or pull Shade toward him and make Pathwell's approach increasingly difficult for Shade to ignore. This may support Shade's confidence that Pathwell is coming without giving him the details of the plan.
- Preserve the distinction: **the draw tells Shade where his compulsion is pulling him; Shade's understanding of what Pathwell is likely to do comes from pattern recognition.**
- Current Chapter 12's exact claim that Pathwell is going to `cut me loose`/that Shade knows the specific solution in advance is stale under this lock.
- Pathwell should reveal the actual absorption/reintegration proposal himself later.
