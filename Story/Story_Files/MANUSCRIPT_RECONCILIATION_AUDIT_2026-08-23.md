# Pathwell Manuscript Reconciliation Audit — 2026-08-23

Status: **AUDIT ONLY — NO MANUSCRIPT CHAPTERS EDITED**

This audit compares every current manuscript file in `Story/Chapters/` against the locked story Bible, especially `canon.md`, `world_bible.md`, `character_bible.md`, and the dated Bible decisions through 2026-08-23.

The goal is not to regenerate the book. The goal is to identify what is stale, what is contradictory, what depends on another fix, and what prose/structure should be preserved when reconciliation begins.

## Legend

- **LIGHT** — preserve almost all prose; small continuity or terminology fixes.
- **MODERATE** — scene works, but one or more mechanics/character beats must be rebuilt.
- **HEAVY** — central scene logic is based on superseded canon; preserve selected prose/beats while rebuilding the sequence.
- **CORE REBUILD** — the existing scene is useful as raw material, but the causal structure must be replaced.

# Global findings

## 1. Object continuity must be fixed before prose edits fan outward

The manuscript currently has overlapping/stale versions of the diary and cookbook.

### Intended object ledger

1. **Chapter 1:** Elizabeth owns the family cookbook and diary. Pathwell enters looking for charged material. He activates one cookbook page; Elizabeth pulls the book away after activation. By the time they leave, Pathwell must clearly have possession of the cookbook and diary so Chapter 3's reclaim works.
2. **Chapter 2:** Pathwell still has both. Elizabeth has not yet completed the reclaim.
3. **Chapter 3:** Elizabeth deliberately reclaims the diary and cookbook, then chooses the Space Between threshold.
4. **Chapter 4:** Elizabeth permanently surrenders the family cookbook at Camp to heal the younger injured woman. A page may be consumed by the working, but the book is no longer Elizabeth's afterward.
5. **Chapters 5–11:** Elizabeth still possesses the diary.
6. **Chapter 12:** Elizabeth voluntarily donates the diary to the Camp archive. Ownership transfers to the archive.
7. **Chapter 12b:** Before confronting Shade, Pathwell legitimately buys the diary back from the archive with a clean prune. He physically possesses it when the confrontation begins.
8. **Archive fire:** the diary becomes physically recoverable by Elizabeth; she chooses the endangered child instead. The ordinary fire destroys the diary.
9. **Coda:** neither original family object remains with Elizabeth.

### Current stale object problems

- Chapter 2 lets Elizabeth physically thumb through the diary even though Chapter 3 later places it back in Pathwell's stack with no clean transfer.
- Chapters 2, 3, and 12b describe the diary as **charred / missing a page**, even though the Chapter 1 consumed page belongs to the **cookbook**, not the diary. This appears to be residue from the discarded diary-as-magic draft and should be removed unless a separate non-magical damage event is deliberately established.
- Chapter 3 appears to distinguish the `old cookbook` Pathwell hands back from `her copy of The Joy of Cooking` in his stack, while the coda treats the surrendered family cookbook as the older *Joy of Cooking*. Reconciliation should establish whether these are one object. Current canon/coda logic strongly favors **one family copy of The Joy of Cooking, heavily annotated by Nana**, not two competing family cookbooks.

## 2. Blob causality must stay consistent across Chapters 1 and 7

- Blobs are cleanup creatures for unclaimed magical waste.
- Elizabeth is not marked, chosen, magical, or the true cleanup target.
- Chapter 1 blob arrives because of the effectively overlapping Pathwell/Shade cleanup signature; the later interrupted cookbook working creates fresh local casting waste that explains the lunge toward Pathwell's active page.
- Chapter 7 cannot say the blob simply `went for Elizabeth` as prey. Elizabeth may become caught in the cleanup event, but the causal target must remain the Pathwell/Shade situation or nearby unclaimed waste.

## 3. Shade's origin must be rewritten once and then propagated consistently

The stale manuscript model repeatedly suggests Pathwell edited/selectively removed futures and accidentally cut Shade out of reality. That is no longer the system.

Locked version:

- A person asked Pathwell for help.
- They did **not** ask him to prune.
- Pathwell chose pruning because it was the quickest/cleanest solution in his mind.
- The prune malformed after release; Shade is the unprecedented person-like consequence/anomaly produced by that failure.
- Pruning is currency, not future selection or reality editing.
- Shade is not an alternate-timeline Pathwell and was not deliberately `decided out of existence`.
- The exact original practical problem remains iceberg lore unless needed.

Chapters 9–12 must all use the same version.

## 4. Shade's information channel is limited

Shade receives no ongoing Pathwell memories, current thoughts, or Elizabeth-specific information through the draw.

He may know things through:

- creation-time fragments/impressions,
- his own observation,
- inference,
- direct conversation,
- the continuing draw/signature resonance and pruning pain.

Any line implying Pathwell has recently `described` Elizabeth to Shade or that Shade knows Pathwell's current plan telepathically must be removed or re-sourced.

## 5. The crash → diner reveal must be restored

The locked beat is: **wrongness rises until Elizabeth realizes in the diner that the man with her is not Pathwell and that she was never the point.**

Current Chapter 8 explicitly has Elizabeth identify the impostor and receive the name `Mr. Shade` before the diner. That destroys the intended Chapter 9 reveal. Chapters 8 and 9 must therefore be treated as a linked rewrite unit.

## 6. Time-of-day continuity needs a final ledger

The likely sequence is:

- Chapter 1: Thursday night.
- Chapters 2–4: same night / early following period.
- Chapter 5–7: following day into evening/night.
- Chapter 8 crash: night.
- Chapter 9 diner: roughly pre-dawn.
- Chapters 10–12b: pre-dawn → first light.

Current Chapter 10 says the nine-o'clock meeting `this morning` has already happened while later chapters still place events before dawn. If the bar/crash occur the evening after the missed meeting, that line should refer to **yesterday morning** or avoid the relative timestamp.

## 7. The Chapter 10 → Chapter 11 transition is missing

Chapter 10 ends with `Camp.` Chapter 11 opens at the museum. The detour/decision/lead that sends them to the museum is absent. This should be repaired before polishing Chapter 11.

## 8. Climax dependency order is now fixed

Do not rewrite Chapter 12b piecemeal. The sequence is:

**diary donation → Pathwell arrives → archive buyback → clean prune / Shade jolt → Pathwell proposes absorption → Shade refuses and holds ground → Pathwell forces contradictory prune → malformed spillage → violent Pathwell-centered recoil → archive ignition → charged-record catastrophe → Elizabeth chooses child over diary → Stansbury fire turn/scar → blob cleanup → Shade eye contact / draw stops / relief / no confession → local blob sweep and departure → aftermath.**

## 9. Coda must be rewritten last

The coda's central meaning changed from `I cannot prune anymore` to **`I can do this, and I stopped.`** It should be reconciled only after the climax is stable.
