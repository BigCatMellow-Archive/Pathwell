## Existing-story check: Chapter 10 drives the wrecked Cadillac after the midpoint crash

Current `Chapter_08.txt` has Elizabeth deliberately turn the car into a telephone pole. The crash is severe: the car wraps around the pole, the pole is through the front of the vehicle, a window/door is compromised, and the vehicle is left at the wreck site.

Current `Chapter_10.txt`, only a short story interval later, has Stansbury's same maroon Cadillac pull over beside Elizabeth while she walks from the diner. The prose itself says the front end is riding low and that the car `had no right to be moving`, but gives no repair mechanism.

A repo search found no lock establishing the Cadillac as supernatural, self-repairing, magically repaired, or otherwise capable of returning to service immediately after that crash.

Existing support already provides a separate post-crash locating mechanism for Pathwell/Stansbury through the longing-letter / grocery-list tracking sequence, so fixing the car does not need to solve how they know where Elizabeth is. The remaining issue is transport once they locate/intercept her.

The midpoint crash is one of Elizabeth's major agency-with-consequence beats. Letting the destroyed car simply keep functioning weakens that consequence and introduces unexplained repair magic.

## Audit Question 115 — What should Pathwell and Stansbury use for transport after the Cadillac is wrecked?

### A. Leave the Cadillac wrecked and have them obtain/borrow another ordinary vehicle before intercepting Elizabeth.

The midpoint crash has a lasting physical consequence. The replacement vehicle need not become a new recurring prop or receive much explanation; it only needs to be plausibly mundane. The established tracking sequence can still get them to Elizabeth, and Chapter 10's Ask can happen in the replacement car.

### B. Leave the Cadillac wrecked and have Pathwell/Stansbury intercept Elizabeth without a car, then use thresholds/other magical travel toward Camp.

This avoids a replacement vehicle but changes the existing car-based Ask scene and introduces more travel mechanics around a conversation that currently benefits from being enclosed in a moving car.

### C. Establish that Stansbury magically repairs or temporarily stabilizes the Cadillac enough to drive.

This preserves the recurring car but adds a new repair capability and immediately erases much of the concrete consequence of Elizabeth's crash.

### D. Keep the Cadillac driving as written without explanation.

The manuscript can lampshade the impossibility, but that does not resolve it under the current grounded magic rules.

### Audit lean: **A**

A preserves both major functions already in place: Elizabeth's crash permanently matters, and the Ask can still occur in the contained moving-car setting. It requires only ordinary replacement transport rather than new magic. B is possible but disrupts a useful scene container; C weakens consequence and invents repair magic; D leaves a causal gap.
