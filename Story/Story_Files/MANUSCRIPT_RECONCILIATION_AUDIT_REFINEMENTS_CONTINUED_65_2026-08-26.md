## Q134 — Elizabeth and Pathwell rebuild enough trust through shared restitution work for her final invitation to be a genuine choice — LOCKED B

- During the three-week restitution interval, Elizabeth and Pathwell spend some time working alongside each other on Camp-directed tasks rather than remaining completely separate until the coda.
- This is **not** a reconciliation montage, romanticized forgiveness, or a return to their earlier dynamic.
- Elizabeth observes Pathwell beginning to behave differently in small, practical ways while the work is happening: asking instead of assuming, accepting `no`, allowing other people to define their own needs, following someone else's plan, leaving capable people to handle their own part, and accepting when an irreplaceable loss cannot legitimately be fixed.
- Pathwell's changed behavior matters more than speeches. He does not persuade Elizabeth to trust him again, demand forgiveness, explain her feelings back to her, or use restitution as leverage for renewed closeness.
- Elizabeth can remain irritated, guarded, or uncertain about him during this period. Their relationship becomes **possible again**, not magically repaired.
- The coda's offhand admission of fault remains understated and should land after Elizabeth has already seen behavioral evidence of change; do not replace it with a full apology/reconciliation scene.
- Elizabeth's final `Are you ready?` invitation remains entirely her choice. She is not following because Pathwell possesses something of hers, claims she is important, needs rescuing, or has convinced her to come.
- The intended meaning is that Elizabeth now knows Pathwell much more clearly — including the harm he caused — has seen enough evidence that he is beginning to change how he acts, and independently decides that she still wants the next adventure.
- This prevents the three-week jump from reading as `time passed, therefore everything is fine` and prevents the ending from resetting Elizabeth into the same compliance pattern she began with.
- Exact shared tasks, dialogue, and scene/montage placement remain deferred to manuscript reconciliation.
