# 1. Purpose

This document turns the reconciliation and expansion roadmaps into **chapter-level contracts**.

A contract states what a chapter/movement must accomplish before prose is revised. It does not prescribe exact dialogue, choreography, jokes, or paragraph order unless already locked elsewhere.

Final chapter numbering is intentionally provisional. Some movements may merge or split during drafting if the causal/value turns demand it. What may **not** change casually is the contract each movement must fulfill.

Every contract is tested against:

1. active Q1–Q149 decisions;
2. current world rules;
3. active character/agency locks;
4. `Therefore/But — Never And Then`;
5. the six-question Universal Chapter Necessity Test;
6. memory-before-loss;
7. POV discipline;
8. magic legality;
9. tone: **wonder + wit + human mess + consequence**.

---

# 2. Contract fields

Each movement tracks:

- **Disposition** — KEEP / EXPAND / REWRITE / SPLIT / NEW / MERGE / CUT.
- **Source material** — current chapter(s) that can be mined for compatible prose/staging.
- **Entrance state** — what must already be true when the chapter begins.
- **Primary want / agency owner** — whose choice drives the chapter.
- **Therefore / But** — causal entrance and complication.
- **Value turn** — what changes emotionally or practically.
- **Exit state** — what must be true to force the next movement.
- **Magic legality** — relevant rules that cannot be violated.
- **Object/continuity state** — important physical continuity.
- **Setup/payoff jobs** — later beats this chapter earns.
- **Pleasure / tone job** — why the chapter is enjoyable to inhabit.
- **Loss-memory job** — what the reader is allowed to have before later loss.
- **Stale material to remove** — discovery-draft mechanics that must not survive.
- **Necessity verdict** — why the chapter survives the six-question test.

## W01 — Apartment / first blob

**Disposition:** REWRITE IN PLACE.

**Source material:** Chapter 1 opening image, sugar-cookie sensory material, intrusive Pathwell energy, bathroom retreat, `Ready? / No. / Perfect.` can all be mined if reconciled.

**Entrance state:** Elizabeth is in a recently occupied apartment, mid-transition in ordinary life. Shade already exists and has been following Pathwell via the draw. Pathwell has felt intermittent unexplained tension since the malformed prune but does not know Shade exists.

**Primary want / agency owner:** Pathwell initially drives action because he wants a useful charged record; Elizabeth wants him out / wants control of her own home.

**Therefore:** Pathwell is scavenging for emotionally charged material and enters Elizabeth's apartment.

**But:** a cleanup blob arrives following the overlapping Pathwell/Shade unresolved signal.

**Therefore:** Pathwell misreads the blob's arrival as evidence that Elizabeth/her apartment is the problem.

**But:** Elizabeth physically interrupts Pathwell's already-active cookbook working, creating fresh local casting waste; once inside, the blob has a legitimate cleanup target near the working.

**Value turn:** Elizabeth's home changes from ordinary-but-unsettled refuge to unsafe/violated space; Pathwell changes from bizarre intruder to apparently necessary guide without actually earning that authority.

**Exit state:** Elizabeth leaves because the situation is genuinely dangerous and Pathwell projects exaggerated certainty around the `marked apartment` explanation. She has not chosen his world yet.

**Magic legality:** anyone can perceive magic; Elizabeth is not special; emotional-text activation requires direct contact with original; page commits once activated; interruption cannot save activated page; blob is cleanup, not predator; successful magic is not what drew it initially.

**Object state:** Nana cookbook remains physically with Pathwell after the working; Elizabeth's diary may also be taken/retained by Pathwell as current property leverage, but it must remain intact and ordinary — no stale charred/missing-page magical history.

**Setup/payoff jobs:** establish charged text sensorially; establish blobs incorrectly interpreted by Pathwell; begin Pathwell's `I understand the problem` flaw; establish Elizabeth being pulled.

**Pleasure/tone job:** fast, strange, tactile, absurdly practical Pathwell under pressure; `Perfect.` works because the crisis has enough breath immediately before the tag.

**Loss-memory job:** give us Elizabeth's ordinary apartment/life enough to understand what is being disrupted; Nana's cookbook must feel lived rather than merely powerful.

**Stale material to remove:** blob explicitly hunting Elizabeth; apartment literally and objectively `marked` as a settled rule; any implication Elizabeth's perception is magical; any diary damage/history contradicting later locks.

**Necessity verdict:** PASS — opening promise, core magic calibration, Pathwell flaw seed, Elizabeth's pulled state, later blob reversal.
