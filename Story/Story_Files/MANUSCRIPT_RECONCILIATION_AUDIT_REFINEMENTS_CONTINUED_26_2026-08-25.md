## Existing-story check: the climax still carries an unsupported charged letter into the reintegration working

Current `Chapter_12b.txt` has Pathwell pull out `a letter, unopened, taken as payment once and never spent`, warm it, and form the cut from it immediately before the failed absorption/reintegration attempt.

Newer canon now defines the climax more precisely:

- pruning is currency, not a free-standing reality-editing spell;
- future possibility must be accepted by an entity, system, or properly constructed magical working;
- the climax is specifically Pathwell trying to pay for a working that would absorb/reintegrate Shade;
- the post-release failure is caused by Pathwell's fundamental contradiction in intent;
- the violent recoil and pruning spillage should therefore read cleanly as consequences of the failed prune-funded reintegration working.

The old letter is no longer automatically justified. Under the emotional-text rules, a charged record can only be used with its emotional/meaningful grain. An unspecified unopened letter cannot simply be generic magical fuel. Keeping it would require defining why its meaning supports reintegration and would also mix emotional-text casting into a climax whose core failure is currently meant to remain a pruning failure.

The world bible already permits a **properly constructed magical working** to accept pruning directly as payment. It does not require such a working to consume emotional text.

## Audit Question 87 — What should provide the magical framework for Pathwell's forced reintegration attempt?

### A. Remove the charged letter; Pathwell constructs a dedicated reintegration working that accepts pruning as payment.

The visible magical form/technique can remain concise and partially unexplained, but no emotionally charged record is consumed. Pathwell builds the working, commits future possibility to it, Shade refuses, Pathwell forces release despite his contradiction, and the payment fails to settle.

### B. Keep a charged handwritten record, but make its authentic grain specifically about reunion / return / bringing separated people back together.

The text supplies a structural/emotional frame while pruning supplies the payment. This can be symbolically pointed, but it introduces a second magical resource and potentially emotional-text casting waste into an already mechanically dense climax.

### C. Use a surviving object or record from Shade's original creation event as the reintegration anchor.

This would make the attempt feel causally tied to Shade's origin, but no such surviving object is currently established. It would require adding new provenance and preserving that object through the story.

### D. Keep the current unspecified unopened letter exactly as-is.

This preserves draft staging but conflicts with the locked rule that emotionally charged text must work with its specific emotion and meaning rather than functioning as interchangeable fuel.

### Audit lean: **A**

A keeps the climax's cause legible: this is a **prune-funded working that Pathwell knowingly forces through a broken intent**. It uses a capability already explicitly permitted by the pruning rules and avoids adding an emotionally charged object whose meaning, provenance, consumption, and waste would all need separate explanation. B is viable if the story specifically wants another symbolic charged record at the climax, but it makes the magic busier and blurs whether the catastrophe is fundamentally a pruning failure or a mixed-text failure. C invents a new continuity burden. D violates the emotional-text grain rule.
