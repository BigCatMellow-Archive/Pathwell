# 2. Definition of DONE

## Finished result

A complete revised Pathwell manuscript in which:

- every chapter obeys the newest active story/Bible/audit locks;
- all stale mechanics and superseded scene logic are removed rather than accidentally preserved;
- character choices remain causally legible;
- the magic system is consistent without becoming over-explained;
- object histories are continuous across chapters;
- POV is overwhelmingly Elizabeth-limited with only the deliberately allowed Pathwell exceptions;
- setup/payoff chains are planted before payoff and do not rely on retroactive exposition;
- consequences survive long enough to matter;
- the climax follows the locked mechanical and moral chain;
- the coda demonstrates changed behavior without claiming complete redemption or erased consequences;
- intentional iceberg lore remains intentionally undefined rather than accidentally contradicted.

## Final proof of DONE

The manuscript is not DONE merely because all chapter files have been rewritten.

DONE requires a final reconciliation proof consisting of:

1. **Lock audit:** every active decision with manuscript impact is mapped to at least one implementing scene or explicit `no manuscript change needed` disposition.
2. **Supersession audit:** every known stale mechanism/beat is confirmed removed or rewritten.
3. **Continuity audit:** characters, injuries, vehicles, thresholds, charged records, cookbook, diary, archive ownership, draw behavior, blobs, pruning, and POV have no unresolved contradictions.
4. **Causal read:** each major scene has a clear `because/therefore` relationship rather than an `and then` patch.
5. **Agency read:** Elizabeth, Pathwell, Shade, and Stansbury make the choices assigned to them by canon; nobody receives agency merely to move plot logistics.
6. **Magic read:** every magical effect has a legal source, purpose, cost, activation path, and aftermath under the current rules.
7. **Cold-reader read:** the manuscript communicates what the reader needs without reproducing the Bible/audit as exposition.
8. **Ending proof:** the final sequence reaches the exact final three lines:
   - `Are you ready?`
   - `No.`
   - `Perfect.`
   with no dialogue after `Perfect.`

## M2 — Pathwell flaw / accountability arc

Core flaw: **convenience dressed as mercy**.

Operational form: **Pathwell turns fixing/helping into control.**

Required escalating pattern:

1. observes real problem;
2. decides he understands what needs to happen;
3. makes himself the mechanism of solution;
4. accepts personal cost and mistakes that cost for moral authority;
5. overrides information, consent, boundaries, or others' definition of the problem;
6. consequences spread beyond what he intended.

Escalation examples:

- enters Elizabeth's apartment while scavenging charged material;
- invents certainty around the `marked apartment` claim;
- self-appoints to solve Camp's book need and uses pruning without being asked;
- Shade's origin arises from the same `help requested, pruning not requested` pattern;
- excludes Elizabeth from information after the bar even after she saves him;
- museum: tries to contain/separate Shade after Elizabeth says she does not need rescuing;
- Camp: Shade says no and Mama Baga says stop; Pathwell forces reintegration anyway.

Required change after catastrophe:

- other people define restitution terms;
- he works without pruning shortcuts;
- accepts irreplaceable losses;
- respects Stansbury's `Let it be`;
- silently leaves another family's cookbook alone rather than treating equivalence as repair;
- can still prune in the coda and chooses to stop before release;
- follows Elizabeth's chosen direction rather than generating the next mission himself.

Failure condition: making Pathwell's personal suffering itself proof of accountability.

## M3 — Pruning

Current story-facing rules:

- currency, not reality editing;
- practitioner spends own future possibility;
- something capable of accepting it must receive it;
- no generic battery;
- future possibility is a living branching structure;
- completed pruning permanently removes branches and their dependent futures;
- life can generate new branches from what remains, but does not restore sold branches;
- a future can be **thin without being short**;
- absolute depletion means death;
- ordinary uncertainty does not block pruning;
- fundamental contradiction in intent is the dangerous failure state;
- trained practitioner can force possibility past safe cancellation, producing malformed post-release spillage;
- early cancellation before release is clean;
- pruning does not require charged text;
- coda: Pathwell remains capable and stops voluntarily before release.

Story introduction/calibration:

- early successful Chapter 3 Camp-book transaction must teach a normal prune clearly enough that the climax failure can be understood as abnormal without an exposition lecture.

Failure condition: slipping back into timeline editing, charged-text pruning fuel, anger-as-force mechanism, or simple lifespan arithmetic.
