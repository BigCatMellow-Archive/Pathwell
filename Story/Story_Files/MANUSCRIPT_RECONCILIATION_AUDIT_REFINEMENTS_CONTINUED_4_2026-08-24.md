## Pathwell explicitly names Shade's draw during the Chapter 10 Ask — LOCKED A

- **Pathwell names the draw during the Chapter 10 Ask after giving Elizabeth the plain causal account of Shade's creation.**
- Elizabeth has already witnessed the draw indirectly at the diner: Shade's attention shifts, his hand tightens, he says `No` to an unseen pull, and then returns to the conversation.
- Until the Ask, Elizabeth does not know what that pull is or that it specifically points toward Pathwell.
- Pathwell now supplies that missing fact: Shade was left with a deterministic compulsion/orientation back toward Pathwell as part of the malformed creation event.
- Pathwell can explain that he did not intend the draw and cannot simply switch it off.
- The draw remains **compulsion, not information**. It gives Shade effectively `Pathwell is that way. Go.` It does not transmit Pathwell's thoughts, current memories, plans, exact distance, or other knowledge.
- Elizabeth's line in the spirit of **`And now he has to come back to you.`** is therefore earned by information Pathwell has just given her rather than by unexplained mechanical intuition.
- This keeps the draw central to Shade's condition before the Camp climax, where it later intensifies physically as Pathwell approaches and surges again when Pathwell performs the clean diary-buyback prune.

## Shade's diner knowledge is limited to observation and inference — LOCKED A

- **Shade is perceptive, not omniscient.** In Chapter 9 he may make sharp judgments from what he has personally seen, what Elizabeth says or does in front of him, and what Pathwell's behavior reveals.
- Shade does **not** receive post-creation Pathwell memories, telepathy, private biographical facts through the draw, or unexplained access to Elizabeth's preferences.
- Remove or reframe exact private claims for which Shade has no legitimate source, including the current certainty around `Boxes packed. You never went back. You loved every minute of it.`
- Preserve the useful underlying character pressure by grounding it in present observation. For example, Shade can point out that Elizabeth has opportunities to leave or disengage once those opportunities genuinely exist, without claiming knowledge of her private history.
- The `collectible` idea may remain only as **Shade's interpretation of Pathwell's observed behavior**, not as secret knowledge of Pathwell's internal thoughts. This is consistent with Shade later inferring that Pathwell acts as though Elizabeth is something he can hold onto.
- The draw remains direction/compulsion only and supplies none of these facts.
