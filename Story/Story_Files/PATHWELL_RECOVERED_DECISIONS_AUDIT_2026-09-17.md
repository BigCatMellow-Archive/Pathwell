# Pathwell — Recovered Decisions Audit — 2026-09-17

Status: **decision-provenance recovery record; not manuscript canon by itself**

Purpose: recover author choices that were made during earlier Pathwell architecture/audit work but were later omitted, blurred into assistant synthesis, or contradicted by stale support files.

This audit exists because a decision can be lost in three different ways:

1. an explicit author choice is never propagated into the main Bible;
2. a later synthesis silently restores an older beat that the author had already removed;
3. an assistant proposal is remembered as though the author selected it.

The goal is not to freeze every historical decision forever. It is to make later changes deliberate.

---

# 1. Provenance and precedence rules

Use this order when records conflict:

1. **A later explicit author choice overrides an earlier author choice.**
2. **An explicit author choice outranks assistant synthesis, recommendation, or reconstruction.**
3. **A later explicit removal remains removed even if an older `canon.md`, roadmap, manuscript, or backward proof still contains the beat.**
4. **A later planning document does not automatically supersede an earlier choice merely because its file date is newer.** Copied stale architecture is not a new decision.
5. **A current experimental architecture may intentionally test a different answer**, but the departure must be named as an experiment rather than silently treated as recovered intent.
6. When authorship cannot be established, classify the item as **support-file lock / provenance uncertain**, not as an author choice.

For future reconciliation, every consequential change should ideally record:

- prior state;
- new state;
- whether the author explicitly selected it;
- what it supersedes;
- whether it is canon, experimental, or still open.

# 5. Magic / world rules that were selected but are underrepresented in V3

## D1. No magical veil

Recovered direct world decision from 2026-08-27:

- magical events are perceptible;
- they are rare enough that the public does not maintain a stable explanatory model;
- people rationalize, forget, fragment accounts, use folklore/religious explanations, or call events coincidence;
- there is no monolithic secrecy spell or cover-up institution required to make the setting work.

**Status:** ACTIVE WORLD RULE.

## D2. Pathwell is centuries old / long-lived; pruning does not spend lifespan directly

Recovered direct world choice from 2026-08-27:

- Pathwell is centuries old.
- Pruning spends future possibility, not units of biological lifespan.
- thinning future possibility need not simply make him die sooner.

**Status:** ACTIVE.

## D3. Pruning does not require charged handwritten text

Recovered from 2026-08-27 Q138=A.

Pruning and emotional-text magic are separate systems. Charged text can be involved in scenes, but is not a universal fuel requirement for pruning.

**Status:** ACTIVE.

## D4. Future possibility is generative but non-restorative

Recovered from 2026-08-27 Q148:

- living/choosing can generate new branches from what remains;
- a branch that was actually pruned does not come back;
- new possibilities are not restoration of the exact sacrificed possibility;
- thinning possibility need not equal shorter lifespan;
- the older audit selected absolute depletion as death, although the exact terminal-prune mechanics were still not fully dramatized.

This is more precise than treating possibility as a simple finite point bank.

**Status:** ACTIVE RULE; terminal edge-case implementation can remain OPEN.

## D5. Pathwell/Shade draw is bidirectional but asymmetric

Recovered from 2026-08-27 Q149:

- Shade experiences the draw as real compulsion/orientation toward Pathwell;
- Pathwell experiences intermittent tension/pull, not an equal compulsion;
- neither receives telepathy, current memories, exact coordinates, or a clean information feed.

Recovered from 2026-08-27 Q131=A:

- Shade's **right hand** is a recurring physical tell when the draw asserts itself.

**Status:** ACTIVE.

## D6. The abnormal cleanup ecology remains specific

Recovered decisions already largely reflected in V3:

- blobs clean unresolved/unclaimed casting/pruning waste rather than ordinary emotion or every magical act;
- physical destruction of dense charged material can produce cascades;
- killing one manifested blob does not eradicate the cleanup ecology;
- the successful cookbook healing should not automatically summon one.

**Status:** ACTIVE.

- **diner recognition had already been chosen;**
- **the tracking spell bridge had already been chosen;**

Future architecture work should treat these as recovered author intent unless the author deliberately changes them now. Current experiments remain allowed; they simply need to be labeled as experiments rather than mistaken for forgotten canon.
