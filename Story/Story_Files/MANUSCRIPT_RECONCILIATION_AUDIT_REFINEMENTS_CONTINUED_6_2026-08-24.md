**Audit Question 57 — How do Pathwell and Stansbury legitimately find Elizabeth after she chooses to leave Shade?**

**A. They are actively searching ordinary roads outward from the crash.** Once able to leave the wreck, they check the limited roads/towns the missing car could plausibly have taken. **B. They first reach the diner through ordinary inquiry, then follow Elizabeth's walking direction.** This would require someone or something to have legitimately identified the diner or Shade's car, and no such information is currently established.

**C. Elizabeth deliberately heads back toward the crash area.** This makes the reunion easier but conflicts with the existing Chapter 10 image of her continuing forward in the direction she had already chosen.

**D. Shade covertly arranges the reunion.** This undermines the new agency lock by making Shade continue managing Elizabeth's next transition and adds coordination not presently supported.

Audit lean: **A**. Chapter 10 already establishes Elizabeth walking along the road in the same direction she had been traveling, while the crash and diner are connected by ordinary roads. A broad road search is sufficient and introduces no new information channel.
