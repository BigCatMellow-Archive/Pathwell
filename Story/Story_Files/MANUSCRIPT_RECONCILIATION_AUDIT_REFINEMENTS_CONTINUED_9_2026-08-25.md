## Audit Question 62 — Where do Pathwell and Stansbury get the tracking materials?

### Context

The tracking spell requires physical handwritten material: one longing/unrequited-love letter to establish the connection, then grocery lists to maintain the track. Elizabeth is already on the road with Shade, so Pathwell and Stansbury cannot spend hours searching for supplies.

### Options

**A. Restore Stansbury's nearby house/library from the older draft.** After getting clear of the wreck, they go to Stansbury's house, which is already established in the older manuscript as nearby. His accumulated paper collection contains the love letter and grocery lists. The Chapter 10 rewind can begin there or on arrival.

**B. Stansbury already carries a small field supply in the Cadillac.** This speeds the sequence up, but changes his established collection from something kept at home into a portable kit and makes specialized magical supplies more conveniently available.

**C. They return to the bar for stored materials.** This keeps them near an already-used location, but the manuscript does not currently establish that the bar stores Stansbury's magical paper collection.

**D. They obtain the materials from the Space Between.** This gives the bookstore another function, but adds travel/portal logistics and makes Stansbury's existing personal collection unnecessary.

### Audit lean: **A**

The older draft already solves both the supply problem and the timing problem: Stansbury has a personal archive, it contains exactly these kinds of mundane handwritten texts, and his house is described as nearby. Restoring that avoids introducing a convenient trunk full of magical supplies or another trip to the Space Between. The cost is that the rewind needs a very short house/library setup before the spell starts, so the scene must stay compressed rather than becoming a second extended location sequence.
