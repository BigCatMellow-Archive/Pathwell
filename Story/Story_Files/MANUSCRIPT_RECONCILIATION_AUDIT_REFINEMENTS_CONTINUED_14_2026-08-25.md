## Existing-story check: source of Shade's stabilization competence

- Question 70 locked Shade as the person who performs the limited charged-letter stabilization working on Elizabeth after the museum injury.
- Existing canon does **not** establish a blanket rule that Shade automatically possesses every magical skill Pathwell possesses.
- The established creation rule does, however, explicitly allow Shade's one-time inheritance to contain **pieces of knowledge**, instincts, habits, and disconnected memories from Pathwell.

## Audit Question 71 — Why does Shade know how to perform the stabilization working?

### Context

At Camp, Shade recognizes that Elizabeth's injured shoulder/upper arm will set badly, waits for her permission, and uses a charged handwritten letter to stabilize it. He explicitly says he is not a healer. The story therefore needs to distinguish this one useful technique from general healing expertise.

### Options

**A. It is one specific creation-time Pathwell knowledge fragment.** Shade inherited a practical `set/hold` emotional-text working or the procedural pieces needed to perform it. He knows how to do this narrow thing without possessing Pathwell's complete magical education or becoming a healer. His own line that he is not a healer remains true.

**B. Shade learned the technique through injuries he personally suffered after creation.** This would make the knowledge independently his, but Shade is recently created and the current story does not establish enough post-creation training or repeated treatment to support that expertise.

**C. The Camp healer talks Shade through the casting in real time.** This avoids inherited magical skill, but raises the question of why the trained healer does not simply perform the working herself and weakens Shade's established intervention.

**D. Shade only knows the physical setting; the Camp healer supplies all magical technique.** Mechanically conservative, but it reopens the just-locked direct stabilization role and removes the charged-letter casting from Shade.

### Audit lean: **A**

A uses the inheritance rule exactly as written rather than broadening it. Shade can possess isolated procedural knowledge without possessing Pathwell's whole magical skill set or even understanding the biography behind the knowledge. That matches his broader borrowed-mannerism design: fragments can be functional without becoming a second Pathwell autobiography. The main guardrail is specificity—this establishes a narrow stabilization technique, not `Shade inherited Pathwell's magic.`
