## Existing-story check: Q111 makes the Space Between exit toward Camp part of Elizabeth's continuation choice

1. Elizabeth follows Pathwell into the Space Between because he still has her diary/cookbook.
2. Pathwell completes the substantial Camp-book purchase and settles older Space Between debt through a clean prune.
3. Elizabeth gets her diary/cookbook back.
4. She puts them atop a stack of the Camp books she is to help carry.
5. She chooses to continue onward with Pathwell toward Camp.

Current `Chapter_03.txt` ends its visible Space Between material at the transaction rather than establishing the route onward. `Chapter_04.txt` begins with Elizabeth and Pathwell arriving at Camp. Therefore the revised agency beat needs a legible physical route between those scenes.

Existing world rules already support:

- the Space Between as an archive with a layered crossroads function;
- thresholds connecting it to multiple places;
- Camp Cunnan being reachable through a mixture of guides, known routes, signs, and established thresholds;
- established anchors being more reliable than temporary/improvised thresholds;
- thresholds not necessarily being two-way.

The remaining design issue is **how direct** the Space Between-to-Camp connection should be. A permanent door directly into Camp risks making Camp's movement/geographic uncertainty feel too easy. A threshold that gets them to a Camp-adjacent approach can preserve both the carrying choreography and Camp's moving-place identity.

## Audit Question 114 — How should Pathwell and Elizabeth travel from the Space Between to Camp with the purchased books?

### A. Use an established Space Between threshold that exits near Camp's current approach/edge, not directly inside Camp.

Pathwell takes up his share of the books and heads through a known threshold. Elizabeth, now holding her recovered diary/cookbook atop her Camp-book stack, chooses to follow. The threshold gets them close enough that Chapter 4 can begin on the final ordinary approach into Camp.

This uses existing crossroads/threshold canon without turning the Space Between into effortless door-to-door transport. Camp still has an edge, an approach, and a moving-place quality; the connection can be one of the durable anchors that tends to reconnect when Camp shifts.

### B. Use a threshold that opens directly inside Camp.

This is the simplest carrying logistics and is allowed by the broad threshold rules. However, it makes the moving Camp considerably easier to access and weakens the sense that reaching it requires practical knowledge of its routes and edges.

### C. Exit the Space Between back into the ordinary world and travel the rest of the way by mundane transport/walking, mostly off-page.

This preserves Camp's geographic elusiveness but adds an otherwise unnecessary travel interval between the agency choice and Chapter 4. It also makes carrying a substantial stack of books more cumbersome without adding much story value.

### D. Leave the route entirely off-page and cut from Elizabeth choosing to continue to the Chapter 4 Camp arrival.

This is economical, but because Q111 specifically makes the Camp-book stack the physical object she chooses to carry onward, an unexplained cut risks making that staging feel discontinuous.

### Audit lean: **A**

A uses rules the story already has and preserves both functions of the scene. The threshold is practical enough to explain how they move the books, but it deposits them at the **approach** rather than erasing Camp's boundaries. Most importantly, Elizabeth's choice can remain physical and immediate: she has her belongings back, Pathwell leaves through the onward threshold with the Camp books, and she chooses to pick up her load and follow.
