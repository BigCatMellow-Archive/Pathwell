## Audit Question 75 — Where should the final Pathwell/Shade confrontation happen now that the diary buyback is gone?

### Context

Pathwell arrives at Camp to confront Shade. Elizabeth has already chosen to witness the decision. The archive must ignite as a direct physical consequence of Pathwell forcing the contradictory reintegration prune after Shade refuses, and the child must already be inside on an ordinary archive errand. Without the diary purchase, Pathwell has no existing reason to walk specifically to the archive first.

### Options

**A. Restore the central-fire confrontation and place the archive among the nearest ring wagons.** Pathwell and Shade confront each other at the central gathering area / edge of the firelight, as the current Chapter 12b already does. The archive is one of the nearby wagons in the established ring, close enough that the malformed recoil directly catches it. This requires only a clear spatial cue before the working begins.

**B. Keep the confrontation directly outside the archive by giving Pathwell another archive errand.** For example, he needs a charged record or component for the reintegration working. This preserves the old proximity lock but invents a new reason for him to visit the archive after the diary-buyback reason has been removed.

**C. Keep Shade at the tree line and let the recoil travel across Camp to ignite the archive.** This preserves some Chapter 12 staging but recreates the long-distance causal problem the earlier audit deliberately removed.

**D. Move the archive to the edge/tree line near Shade.** This solves distance by changing Camp geography primarily for the climax and has little existing-story support.

### Audit lean: **A**

A recovers existing manuscript staging and existing Camp geography instead of replacing the deleted diary errand with another plot device. The central fire is a natural place for an unavoidable public confrontation in a ring-shaped camp, and Elizabeth's witness role fits there. The key constraint is proximity: the archive should be explicitly one of the nearest wagons on the ring so the recoil ignites it directly rather than behaving like a targeted magical projectile. B works but risks inventing a second archive pretext immediately after deciding the first one was unnecessary. C reopens the distance problem; D reshapes geography solely for convenience.
