# Pathwell — Terminology Correction: Elizabeth's Self-Direction — 2026-09-17

Status: **current planning-language correction; applies to experimental/revision notes**

Purpose: remove ambiguity created by using `authorship` as metaphorical shorthand for Elizabeth's character arc in a story where literal writing can carry magic.

## Correction

Where current September 17 planning documents describe Elizabeth's growth as:

- `authorship`;
- `becoming the author` of her direction;
- `authorship increasing`;
- similar metaphorical wording;

read that language as:

> **self-direction**

or, where the sentence is specifically about a decision:

> **decision ownership**

This is a terminology correction, not a change to Elizabeth's arc.

## Preferred arc wording

> **adaptation -> preference -> voluntary continuation -> refusal -> deliberate action -> ownership of consequence -> self-direction**

## Hard mechanical clarification

Elizabeth remains entirely nonmagical.

She does **not**:

- create magic by writing;
- write commands/destinations that reality obeys;
- activate charged handwritten material through her own practitioner ability;
- become a practitioner;
- discover latent magical power;
- independently perform pruning;
- independently operate a magical threshold through personal magic;
- become cosmically selected because she is the protagonist.

She may:

- choose a destination;
- ask a practitioner to open a route;
- use an already-imbued object designed for non-practitioner use;
- physically act in a magical environment;
- make decisions that magical characters then help execute;
- refuse, request, consent, intervene physically, rescue people, and accept consequences without magical power.

The important distinction is:

> **Needing somebody else's magical capability does not give that person ownership of Elizabeth's decision.**

Example: Elizabeth may choose Camp and ask Shade/Pathwell to open the magical route. The destination is her decision; the magical mechanism is someone else's capability.

This correction applies prospectively to planning language in files such as:

New work should use `self-direction` consistently.
