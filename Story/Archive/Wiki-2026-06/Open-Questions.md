## Active Structural Gaps

### The Diary's Placement
The diary's role in the narrative remains floating. It functions as a tether to Elizabeth's past self (not a weapon), and it burns in the archive fire — she runs past it. But its exact placement and the mechanics of how it moves through the story need consistent execution.

### Shade's Blind Spot
Shade currently lacks a blind spot, making him too correct and reducing his narrative function. He needs to be wrong about something — or at least limited in a way that matters.

### Act III Blank (Diner → Museum)
There's a structural blank between the diner and the museum that needs solving. Elizabeth must make at least one meaningful choice while with Shade. She cannot just be transported.

### Stansbury's Interiority
Stansbury has underdeveloped interiority and motivation beyond his functional role. His leadership arc needs to feel earned, not stumbled into. The "keeps people small" flaw must be established before the fire moment.

### Shade's Emotional Development
Currently insufficient for his death scene to land. The reader needs to feel something when Shade dies — that requires more groundwork earlier.

### Mama Baga's Relationship to Pathwell
"Pathwell's adopted mother" is established but the specifics are an unresolved structural gap. What does that relationship look like? What does it cost him? Why does it matter to the reader before the healing scene?
