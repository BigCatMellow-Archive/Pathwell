# Elizabeth

**The protagonist. Passive → Active.**

## At a Glance

| | |
|---|---|
| **Start state** | Passive, directionless, numb |
| **Core want** | To matter — to anyone, including herself |
| **Core flaw** | She drifts. Comfort is her trap. |
| **Lie** | "My choices don't matter." |
| **Truth** | "Every choice matters." / "Choosing is what makes you human." |
| **Arc** | Positive change. Someone life happens to → someone who happens to life. |

## Starting Condition

She didn't care enough to be phased by a man breaking into her apartment. She had a routine and didn't notice the world around her. She isn't frozen by fear of choosing wrong — she's frozen by the belief that her choices don't change anything. She doesn't think she matters.

She's suppressing the need to be significant.

## Voice

Focuses on the wrong thing under pressure. Her bizarre priorities ARE her characterization.

- "You better plan on cleaning all this up" — while something slams against the door
- "Do you mind?" — to an intruder

## The Agency Ladder

Each rung costs more than the last. See [[Agency Ladder]] for full breakdown.

1. **Lo mein (Ch2)** — Chooses to stay. Costs nothing material.
2. **Books on the stack, through the door (Ch3)** — Chooses uncomfortable over comfortable. Costs her job, meeting, routine.
3. **Cookbook to heal Mama Baga (Camp)** — Costs her grandmother's love. Permanent.
4. **Uses her own diary as a weapon** — Costs reliving every moment she felt invisible.
5. **Runs past the burning cookbook to save children** — An action, not a realization.
6. **"Are you ready?" (Ending)** — She initiates. The roles have flipped.

## Elizabeth and Magic

**The key discovery:** In a world where everyone uses OTHER people's emotions as power, Elizabeth is the only person who uses her own. Her diary — her own anger, fear, hurt — becomes her weapon. The cost is reliving everything she felt when she wrote those pages.

The magic system's built-in cost (you feel the emotion of the text) becomes a character crucible. She has to face who she was to become who she's becoming.

⚠️ **Critical thesis constraint:** Elizabeth matters *without* any power source. If she uses magic — including her own emotions as magic — the thesis collapses into empowerment fantasy. The diary is a tether to her past self, not a weapon. It burns in the archive fire and she runs past it.

## Elizabeth CANNOT

- Skip rungs on the agency ladder
- Jump from passive to heroic
- Have magic systems or fortune tellers reveal her true desires
- Learn lessons through realization instead of action
- Go back to comfort without losing her arc — every safe landing must be followed by a choice to leave it

## Comfort Is Her Trap

Every time she settles in somewhere, check: is she becoming a passenger again? Camp Cunnan is comfortable. It could become her new routine. This is dangerous.

## Relationship to Other Characters

- **Pathwell** dragged her into this. Took her cookbook. Brought danger to her door. Every cost she pays traces back to his choices.
- **Shade** cracks her faith in Pathwell with the diner accusation. She stays with him because at least he's honest about what she is to him.
- **Stansbury** tries to keep her safe by limiting her involvement. She pushes back — she's tired of being managed.
