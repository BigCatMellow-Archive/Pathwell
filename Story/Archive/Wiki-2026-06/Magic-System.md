# Magic System

The magic in Pathwell centers on **emotional-text magic** — handwritten documents imbued with human feeling that wizards draw power from.

## Core Rules

1. **Written text carries emotional "charge"** from whoever wrote it.
2. **Using text transfers the emotion** into the user (side effect: you feel what's written).
3. The transferred emotion can be **shaped into magical effects**.
4. **Using a page consumes it**, but the user retains the knowledge/content briefly.
5. **Handwritten only.** This rule is sacred.

## Emotional Categories → Effects

| Emotion | Sources | Effects |
|---|---|---|
| Love/joy | Cookbooks, love letters, wedding vows | Protection, healing |
| Anger/hate | Hate mail, angry letters | Destruction, force |
| Fear/anxiety | Diaries, doomsday journals | Defensive, draining |
| Grief/sorrow | — | Binding, weighing down |
| Wonder/curiosity | — | Revelation, illumination |

## Key Established Moments

- **Grandmother's cookbook** → golden protective light, taste of sugar cookies, grandmother's voice. This is the benchmark scene for all worldbuilding.
- **Love note** → wound closes, lingering feeling of a boy from middle school.
- **Diary (anger/fear/hurt)** → destructive potential.

## The Cardinal Rule of Depicting Magic

**The strongest moments are sensory, not explanatory.** Sugar cookies > classification charts. The body knows first — goosebumps in the fear section, warmth near love, a flinch when magic costs something.

## Imbuing

[[Stansbury]] can transfer emotional charge into objects (Nerf weapons). This represents his character — wants to arm people but can't bring himself to give them real weapons.

## Organization

[[Locations#the-space-between|The Space Between]] bookstore organizes texts on a modified Dewey Decimal system by emotional spectrum and intensity.

⚠️ **Critical revision rule:** The Dewey Decimal classification must **NOT** become a lecture. Elizabeth's body tells the reader what section she's in. This is the biggest violation of the "write it like the sequel" principle in the discovery draft.

## Related Systems

- [[Blobs]] — interdimensional predators drawn to pruned signatures

## Elizabeth's Unique Relationship to Magic

In a world where everyone uses OTHER people's emotions as power, [[Elizabeth]] is the only person who uses her own. Her diary — her own anger, fear, hurt — becomes her weapon. Everyone else borrows. She doesn't.

⚠️ **Thesis constraint:** See [[Thesis and Controlling Ideas]]. Elizabeth must matter *without* any power source. The diary is a tether to her past self, not a weapon.
