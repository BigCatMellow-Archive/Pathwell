### Therefore / But chain

Three weeks of restitution have produced behavioral evidence **therefore** Elizabeth can choose to seek strangeness again without that choice functioning as automatic forgiveness; **but** returning to wonder must still test whether Pathwell's old fixing reflex remains active, so the Space Between presents a genuine family cookbook that looks categorically capable of filling a loss. The Merritt book is authentic **but** authentic histories are not interchangeable; therefore Pathwell's initial acquisition impulse reaches all the way to familiar pruning preparation before he recognizes the false equivalence. He remains fully capable of releasing future possibility **but** the Shopkeeper has not yet accepted it; therefore Pathwell can choose the clean cancellation point and does. The cookbook remains itself, and Camp retains authority over whether it even wants to hear about the listing. Pathwell acknowledges Shade's death as his fault **but** Elizabeth does not convert that admission into absolution. Elizabeth still wants to go somewhere **therefore** she asks about available routes; **but** Pathwell only provides options, so Elizabeth chooses one herself. She then asks whether Pathwell wants to accompany her rather than assuming his participation. Both choose the route separately **therefore** the opening `No / Perfect` exchange can return with reversed meaning: unreadiness is no longer coerced momentum, but chosen uncertainty.

### Value turn

Pathwell's final growth is restraint without incapacity: he sees a solvable-looking absence, begins the exact old shortcut, remains able to pay with his future, and voluntarily stops before release because he recognizes that neither the object nor the decision is his to substitute. Elizabeth's final growth is initiation without magical empowerment: she chooses the Space Between, asks for practical route information, selects the next destination, and invites Pathwell to accompany her.

Opening uncertainty meant Elizabeth being pulled through events she did not choose. It means both characters can enter uncertainty without pretending certainty is required.
