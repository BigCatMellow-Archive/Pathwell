## Short description

An informed review against the author's voice guide over-cuts. Lines that state a feeling or name a choice look like "explaining what the scene just showed" to a reviewer holding the guide. Readers who meet them cold often find those same lines the most moving in the book.

## Trigger

EXP-0023 (P87): three informed reviewers each read the whole book through one role of the web and proposed 19 cuts of narrator commentary and stated theme. The next day, two blind readers read the whole book with no goals, logging reactions as they went (P88). They singled out 11 of those lines as moving or good. Examples: "The choice was ridiculous. / It was still hers." ("Tiny, perfect", both readers); "That didn't make the next step his." ("Moved", both); the Ch8 ending ("Excellent ending", both). Only the lines no reader mentioned stayed as cuts.

## The lesson

The guide warns against explanation by *habit*. A reader's response is the evidence for whether a given line is habit or earned. Before proposing to cut a line, search the blind-read logs in `Story/Revision/Reader-Reports/` for it. If a blind reader named it as moving, keep it unless a ruling says otherwise, and say so in the candidate.
