# Wording the boxes

A box title is read in a cell about a hundred pixels wide, next to seven others. It has to say what the box does for the box above it, in a sentence someone would say out loud.

## The test

Read only the titles on one sheet. For each one, ask: *could a stranger tell what this box does for the one in the middle?* If the answer is "it tells me where I am in the book" or "it's the quote's first word", rewrite it.

## Patterns

| Use | Avoid |
| --- | --- |
| A subject and a verb: "He yells for help" | A label: "Help!" |
| What it does: "Chapters 1, 3 and 4 depend on the tear" | Where it is: "Ch1 depends on it" |
| The problem, stated: "Where the pile came from isn't shown" | The object: "The pile" |
| The meaning: "Nobody at work has noticed her" | The event: "Joke about the landlord" |
| An actor by what they stand for: "Shade: the person someone else's choice made" | A bare name: "Shade" |
| A facet with its prefix: "Wants: her books back, then the truth" | A facet as a category: "Motivation" |
| A plain sentence: "He lets Shade be taken" | A mannered one: "Silence, not salvation" |

## Real replacements

From the Pathwell chart, v2 → v3. The v2 titles were all accurate; none of them said anything.

| v2 (label) | v3 (statement) | Why it's better |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The last page earns it | Choose your own life, ready or not, and let other people choose theirs | The center is now the message, not a description of the ending's job. |
| Elizabeth chooses her way here | Elizabeth: the one who learns to choose | An actor named by what she stands for, opening into eight facets. |
| Seen first | Something was at her door before any magic | Says why the moment matters: it's the clue. |
| Assumes she knows | He thought she'd know | Plain and in his point of view. |
| Recalculates | *(cut)* | It only restated the quote ("Huh."). No job, no box. |
| The pile | Where the pile came from isn't shown | Names the problem the box exists to track. |
| Help! | He yells for help | A quote as a title tells you nothing until you've read the book. |
| Ch1 depends on it | Chapters 1, 3 and 4 depend on the tear | Says the scope and the dependency. |
| The price, felt | It hurts like it should | Says what the moment proves. |

## Notes ("How it gets us there")

The note is one or two sentences under the title. It says how this box gets to the box above, or why it matters, in the same plain register.

- Good: *"If Shade went quietly, his death would be his own choice and Pathwell's silence would cost less. He fights, so it doesn't."*
- Weak: *"Shade's struggle is thematically significant."* (True of anything; says nothing.)

## Proposed wording

Candidate text for the source goes in `proposed`, never in `quote` or `title`. Title the box by what the option is ("B3: “tore free and”", "C2: show the dust"), and put why in the note ("Recommended. Three words, no new magic.").
