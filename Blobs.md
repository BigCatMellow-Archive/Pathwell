# Blobs

**Interdimensional "cleanup" predators.**

## Behavior

- Sense [[Pruning|pruned signatures]] as "food"
- Can't distinguish [[Pathwell (Character)|Pathwell]] from [[Shade]] — pursue both
- After tasting [[Elizabeth]], begin pursuing her too
- Amorphous, at least 6 feet, stench of wet-rot/low tide

## Plot Function

The blobs are the pressure mechanism that keeps the story moving. They escalate consequences and force characters into choices they'd rather avoid.

Key moments:
- **Chapter 1:** Blob attacks Elizabeth's apartment. Pathwell fights it with the cookbook's protection spell.
- **Bar scene:** Blob appears. Elizabeth gets absorbed. She cuts herself out using a Nerf dagger imbued by [[Stansbury]]. This proves she can affect outcomes — but draws the blobs' attention to her specifically. Agency has consequences.
- **Midpoint:** Blobs chasing all of them creates the pressure that leads to the crash.
- **Camp climax:** Blobs arrive sensing Pathwell, Shade, and Elizabeth. They go for immobile Elizabeth.
