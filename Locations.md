# Locations

Locations in Pathwell are characters. A place that could be swapped for any other place isn't earning its page space. If a conversation could happen anywhere, the location isn't working.

---

## The Space Between

**A bookstore that exists between spaces.**

Accessible through magic doors. Contains the emotional text archive organized by modified Dewey Decimal (emotional spectrum + intensity). The [[Supporting Characters#the-shopkeeper|Shopkeeper]] runs it. [[Supporting Characters#boots|Boots]] guards it. Terrible coffee. The air is different — cooler, denser, heavy with accumulated emotion.

**How it should feel:** The magic itself shapes the environment — temperature, smell, sound, emotional pressure. It should feel different from every other bookstore because of what it contains.

### Revision Rule

The threshold crossing into the Space Between is [[Elizabeth]]'s first step into the magical world. It must be:
- **Sensory** — temperature, smell, sound, the sudden absence of street noise
- **Overwhelming** — physical
- **NOT** a tour
- **NOT** a lecture

### Narrative Function

- Where [[Pathwell (Character)|Pathwell]] pays via [[Pruning]]
- Where the Shopkeeper observes: "You don't have much left to give"
- Where Pathwell's private retry fails quietly (the crack of humility)
- Where the final exchange sets up the ending: "Do this for me and you can have the book"

## Camp Cunnan

**Refuge with an emotionally saturated written archive.**

Letters, journals, histories. A community. [[Supporting Characters#mama-baga|Mama Baga]] as elder. Becomes the site where the archive burns — making consequence concrete and permanent.

**How it should feel:** Magic woven into daily life. Not a fortress or a school — a home.

### Elizabeth's Danger at Camp Cunnan

It's comfortable. It could become her new routine. **Comfort is her trap.**

### Narrative Function

- Where Elizabeth meets Mama Baga and the community
- Where she sacrifices the cookbook to heal Mama Baga
- Where Shade infiltrates and is eventually exposed by his draw tell
- Where the archive fire happens — the climax
- Where named artifacts must be established early so they can burn later

## Elizabeth's Apartment

The opening location. Destroyed by the blob attack in Chapter 1. Elizabeth can't go home. This is the inciting incident that removes her safety net.

---

## The Dumpling Stand

Chapter 2's deceleration location. Plastic tables. The site of Elizabeth's first choice (the lo mein). The quiet after the storm.

---

## The Diner

Where [[Shade]] makes his accusation. Greasy spoon. Neon sign reading "Dinner 24/7." Where Elizabeth's faith in Pathwell cracks.

---

## The Museum

Where Elizabeth chooses who she goes with, and why. The dramatic question of Act III resolves here. She rejects BOTH Pathwell's and Shade's framings of her.
