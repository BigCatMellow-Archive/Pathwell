# Pruning

**The currency of the magical world. Characters sell "what may be's" (potential futures) to the Bookkeeper for goods and services.**

## How It Works

- Pruning is **blind** — the seller doesn't choose which futures get pruned.
- Each transaction **narrows variance**, reduces future agency, makes the seller more deterministic.
- It's the mechanism by which [[Pathwell (Character)|Pathwell]] has been running for centuries — and the mechanism that created [[Shade]].

## The Consent Rule (Non-Negotiable)

Pruning requires **full internal consent/confidence**. Doubt cancels the transaction. You cannot lie to yourself into consent.

This rule is sacred and creates automatic drama:
- If it works → he meant it
- If it cancels → he's lying to himself

## Failure Modes

| Type | What Happens |
|---|---|
| **Normal failure** | Simply doesn't work. Transaction cancels. |
| **Catastrophic failure** | Only when someone *forces* it by overriding doubt. Not "fails and explodes" but "forces alignment and tears it." |

## Shade: The Consequence of Pruning

[[Shade]] is a manifested duplicate created by a rare failed pruning. He is Pathwell's pruned futures given form — the lives Pathwell could have lived but sold away. Casual pruning treats Shade's existence as worthless, which is what forces him into the open.

## Pruning in the Plot

1. **Early:** At least one clean, casual prune (not only the dramatic tree ritual). Establishes the rules.
2. **Bookstore:** Pathwell prunes to pay for supplies. The Shopkeeper notes he doesn't have much left.
3. **Camp climax:** Pathwell tries to prune to lure [[Blobs]] away. **Pruning CANCELS** — he doubts his own control for the first time.
4. **Forced:** He injects anger from an intense text to override doubt. The forced pruning **TEARS**. The archive ignites.
5. **Private retry:** At The Space Between, alone. Tries to prune to buy back the cookbook. Fails quietly. And instead of rage — *relief*. The crack of humility.

## Thematic Function

Pruning IS the metaphor for the controlling idea. Selling potential for short-term control. Each transaction makes you more deterministic, less free. [[Pathwell (Character)|Pathwell]]'s entire arc is the consequence of this bargain.
