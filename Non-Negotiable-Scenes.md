# Non-Negotiable Scenes

**These ten scenes must exist in the final manuscript. They are the structural pillars.**

---

1. **At least one clean, casual prune early** — not only the dramatic tree ritual. Establishes the [[Pruning]] rules in action before they become plot-critical.

2. **Elizabeth cuts herself out of blob absorption** — agency proof + [[Blobs|blob]] taste/pursuit established. She can affect outcomes. She's not furniture. But it draws attention to her.

3. **Cookbook sacrificed to heal Mama Baga** — escalating cost, permanent. See [[Agency Ladder]] rung 3.

4. **Midpoint crash** — agency with consequence. Elizabeth grabs the wheel to prove she exists in the car, not to drive it. The crash is unintentional.

5. **Diner accusation** — single sharp point. "He saved you from a mess he brought to your door." See [[Shade]].

6. **Museum choice** — [[Elizabeth]] rejects both framings, chooses for her own reasons. She's not Pathwell's tool and she's not Shade's evidence.

7. **Pruning cancel + anger injection forcing** — the hinge of [[Pathwell (Character)|Pathwell]]'s arc. He doubts → it cancels → he forces it → the archive ignites.

8. **Archive burning** — specific loss. Cookbook, camp histories, named artifacts. Named artifacts must be established early so the burning has weight.

9. **Shade's death** — eye contact with Pathwell, the draw stops, relief without confession. Presence is enough.

10. **Private retry at The Space Between fails quietly** — the humbling hinge. Pathwell tries to prune, it doesn't work, and he feels relief instead of rage.
