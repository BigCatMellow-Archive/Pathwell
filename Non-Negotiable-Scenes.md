# Non-Negotiable Scenes

**These ten scenes must exist in the final manuscript. They are the structural pillars.** The Chapter 14–18 climax beats below reflect the author's coordinated October 15, 2026 revision; older August interview staging remains in decision history.

> **Status, 2026-09-30:** brought into line with the Bible interview (2026-08-21 to 08-27) at the author's direction ([revision Decisions, R14](Story/Revision/Decisions.md#the-authors-rulings-for-this-revision)). The earlier list predated the interview; it's in git history. Each scene names the lock behind it; the climax (8–9) is still being explored ([Q-E](Story/Revision/Decisions.md#open-for-the-author)).

---

1. **At least one clean, casual prune early.** Pathwell buys Camp's books at the Space Between with his own future, a job nobody gave him, and clears an old debt. Establishes pruning as routine before its cost becomes plot-critical. (Q107–Q109)

2. **Elizabeth cuts Pathwell out of the bar blob.** The mirror has turned the problem toward him; the blob takes him, not her, and she chooses to use Stansbury's imbued foam dagger. Stansbury made the tool magical; Elizabeth chose to use it. (Bible decisions 08-23 §6; Q106)

3. **The cookbook given to heal a stranger.** A young woman hurt in a wagon accident at Camp; Elizabeth gives the whole book, one page is spent, and the rest becomes the archive's, with a lived life there before it burns. (Q129)

4. **The midpoint crash, chosen.** Elizabeth deliberately puts the car in the ditch because the brothers keep talking around her. She chooses the crash, not its consequences. "It was the crash that woke her." recurs here, bare. (Refinements §9; the author, 2026-09-30)

5. **The diner.** Elizabeth recognizes that the man isn't Pathwell when he refers to Pathwell as someone else, then learns she was never the point. Shade doesn't hand her a confession: "He knows what he did. Ask him." (Refinements §11–15)

6. **The Ask, and the museum choice.** She makes Pathwell say plainly what he did ("But you still chose it." / "Yes."), chooses Camp because he doesn't control the room there, and tells him "I don't need to be rescued." He overrides her anyway; the discharge destroys the museum's records and hurts her. (Refinements §16–24; Q142–Q146)

7. **The forced reintegration.** Pathwell builds the frame while talking; Shade says no; Mama Baga says stop; he forces the prune through his own contradiction, and the failure's recoil sets the archive on fire. No one else causes the failure for him. (Bible decisions 08-22–23; Q145, Q147)

8. **The archive burning, and Elizabeth's choice.** As Shade is unexpectedly caught by the approaching cleanup creature and cries for help, **both** he and Elizabeth hear Milo calling from the burning archive. They make eye contact; Elizabeth turns to rescue the pinned child with no spell, leaving the enchanted dagger unused. The diary and cookbook are within reach, but she passes both, and they burn in ordinary fire. The blue road notebook and other records remain at risk. (Earlier Bible decisions 08-23 §1, Q130; later author Rung Q51–59 and restrained agency ruling, 2026-10-15)

9. **Shade's death, without consent, and Pathwell's choice.** The failed reintegration's fresh waste draws a blob through Camp's resisting border; it catches Shade by the shin as he tries to help with the fire. He fights and calls for help rather than voluntarily approaching or serenely accepting removal. A Camp member attempts to pull him free; Elizabeth is inside saving Milo. The creature takes Shade and clears the fresh waste; the draw quiets because the anomaly is gone. **Pathwell witnesses Shade's struggle and consciously withholds a call for help** because the creature's removal of Shade appears to solve his problem; this fact is revealed later by his own confession. Camp names Shade. **This supersedes the August Q132 voluntary risk, as directed by the author in October 2026.**

10. **The quiet refusal, later confession and invitation.** Pathwell sees another family's cookbook, wants to buy it to fill Nana's place, and silently puts it back; he chooses not to find out whether he can still prune. **Later that visit**, he confesses from shame that he deliberately did not summon help while Shade struggled, and Elizabeth relates her choice to save Milo; he gently distinguishes them. The existing hour of browsing follows the confession. Elizabeth independently chooses their next destination and invites Pathwell without an explicit pardon; the novel ends on "Are you ready?" / "No." / "Perfect." The specific confession supersedes the old August Q94–Q95 silence under the author's **Version C** direction (2026-10-15).
