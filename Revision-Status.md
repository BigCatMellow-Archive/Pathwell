## Known Recurring Weaknesses

Patterns to watch for during all revision work:

- **Exposition disguised as dialogue** — characters explaining things to each other that they both already know
- **Adding volume when uncertain** — piling on words instead of cutting for clarity
- **Elizabeth's passivity extending past where it serves the narrative** — she should be climbing the ladder, not stalling
- **Dewey Decimal–style magic system lectures** — the biggest violation of "write it like the sequel"
- **Dangling modifiers, over-explanation, listing/cataloging instead of experiencing**
