## Known recurring weaknesses

From the [assessment](Story/Revision/Assessment-2026-09-29.md#book-level-findings), with counts for the current chapters; watch for them in every pass:

- **Explaining what the scene just showed**, including the narrator stating the theme (240 short commentary paragraphs; 2–6 per chapter in Chapters 1–3, up to 30 in Chapter 16).
- **One cadence for every register:** "Not X. / Y." fragments (238) and stacked one-line paragraphs, so the climax sounds like the banter.
- **Narration that doesn't sound like a person talking:** 246 uncontracted forms ("He did not pull away.").
- **One shared deadpan** across the cast; "Fair." is everyone's word.
- **Adding volume when uncertain:** repeated beats in the aftermath chapters (16–17) where one sharper beat would do.
- **Continuity and logistics slips:** see the assessment's list.

No longer current: Elizabeth's passivity (she drives the story from Chapter 8), and the "Dewey Decimal" magic lectures (gone). Exposition disguised as dialogue is now rare.
