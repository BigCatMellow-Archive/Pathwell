# Supporting Characters

## Mama Baga

**Elder of [[Locations#camp-cunnan|Camp Cunnan]]. Pathwell's adopted mother.**

The person [[Elizabeth]] heals with the cookbook. Must be established as a character the reader cares about **BEFORE** she needs healing — otherwise the sacrifice is abstract.

⚠️ **Open problem:** Mama Baga's relationship to [[Pathwell (Character)|Pathwell]] is an unresolved structural gap. See [[Open Questions]].

## The Shopkeeper

**Runs [[Locations#the-space-between|The Space Between]].**

Practiced, dry, transactional. His introduction speech is a legal disclaimer he resents having to give. Cares about Pathwell underneath the professional distance.

Key line: *"You don't have much left to give, Pathwell Shade."*

**Ending function:** Offers the exchange that sets up the final scene. "Do this for me and you can have the book." Elizabeth offers the adventure. Pathwell isn't ready. "Perfect."

## Boots

**The cat. Blood god. Paid in mice.**

Draws blood as entry price to the bookstore. Nobody explains what a blood god is or why a cat qualifies. Pure sequel energy.

An orange tabby. Twelve pounds of paws and claws. Circles you like a shark. His eyes glean fiendishly after drawing blood. He is the "diminutive lord of slaughter."

Boots is the litmus test for whether the story is doing "write it like the sequel" correctly. If anyone explains Boots, something has gone wrong.
